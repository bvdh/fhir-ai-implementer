- **Description**: The existing wording under paragraph §2.42.2 could lead one to believe that any Bundle containing Composition SHALL contain referenced resources from

Composition.subject
Composition.encounter
Composition.author
Composition.attester.party
Composition.custodian
Composition.event.detail
Composition.section.author
Composition.section.focus
Composition.section.entry

After a brief discussion on Zulip it seems that was not the intention. Instead the intention of that text is that these inclusion requirements only apply when you vread/read/search on the Bundle endpoint for type=document or when you explicitly ask for Composition/[id]/$document.

I like this intention because it means that querying/reading Composition behaves largely the same as any other resource. However based on the current wording I misinterpreted that intention and implemented the wrong thing.

Suggest to update wording under §2.42.2 to be more specific about what the SHALL applies to.

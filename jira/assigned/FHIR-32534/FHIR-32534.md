- **Resolution Description**: Change the comment to be the following:
The Last Modified Date on the composition may be after the date of the document was attested without being changed.

This means that the date on an amended document is the date of the amendment, not the date of original authorship.
Modify the requirements of the "date" to:
dateTime is used for tracking, organizing versions and searching. Note that this is the time of authoring this version of the Composition. When packaged in a document, Bundle.timestamp is the date of packaging.

- **Description**: The proposed wording should be added as a usage note for the sake of greater clarity.

Proposed Wording:
This means that the 'date' on an 'amended' document is the date of the amendment, not the data of original authorship. To find the date of original authorship, use Provenance.

- **Resolution Description**: On Bundle.link, we will add a note saying:

"The behavior of navigation link types (next/prev/first/last) are well defined for searchset and history Bundles but are not currently defined for other types. Implementers who choose to use such link relationships for other bundle types will need to negotiate behavior with their interoperability partners."

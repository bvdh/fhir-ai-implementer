- **Description**: Several CapabilityStatements appear to require SMART Backend Services as a mandatory authorization mechanism. This may be too restrictive:

SMART Backend Services shoule be one possible approach, but not the only one
In EU cross-border contexts, authorization may rely on different trust frameworks
Treating SMART as mandatory may conflict with regulatory and architectural flexibility

Proposed Change:
Revise the CapabilityStatements to make SMART Backend Services a supported or preferred option, rather than a strict requirement, e.g.:
“The server SHOULD support SMART Backend Services. Other authorization mechanisms MAY be used, provided they comply with the requirements of this guide and applicable EU regulations.”
Alternatively:

Define SMART as a preferred pattern in narrative text, but not as a SHALL requirement in CapabilityStatements

I think this deserves an in-person group discussion. We've slotted an agenda item on June 10th to discuss this further if you would like to join us. Let us know if not and we can re-schedule.

### 3. Lloyd McKenzie - 2021-05-26

C-CDA isn't the only use for Composition. It could constrain if it wanted to, but in general I think not supporting multiple identifiers even for C-CDA would be a mistake. Just because "it's always been that way in CDA" doesn't mean it should continue to be in FHIR. It's not as though people will go off creating additional identifiers for fun just because the capability exists...

### 4. David Lucas (Inactive) - 2021-05-26

Well we had an exact use case for our modernization of health records project where the client needed to still understand or search by specific site, system, and local identifier which was 3 identifiers that needed to be bound to the composition. We were left with having a one off resources that had these items bound as meta tags where the rest of the FHIR resources behaved a different way.

### 5. Sean McIlvenna - 2021-05-26

I think this primarily comes from the well-established use case of CDA. In CDA there is only a single identifier for a document. GrahameGrieve was part of the discussion today and indicated he couldn't think of a scenario where more would be needed either. I can see your point though...

### 6. Lloyd McKenzie - 2021-05-26

DocumentReference.identifier has the same semantic as Composition.identifier and it is 0..*. Multiple identifiers can be assigned to the same document (simple example is ISBN and UPCs on a book). What is the rationale for NOT supporting multiple identifiers?

### 7. Sean McIlvenna - 2021-05-25

We have decided to move forward changing the mapping to Bundle.identifier as part of another request: FHIR-30766

### 8. Sean McIlvenna - 2021-05-25

Discussed during May WGM. We do not believe that identifier needs to repeat. However, there are some other changes that could be made to improve the mapping of ClinicalDocument.id to FHIR. We're proposing that the 2.41.3.3 Composition section be updated to indicate that ClinicalDocument.id maps to Bundle.identifier. The following change should be made:

" where the Bundle.id is equivalent in function to ClinicalDocument.id (but it is not identical when interconverting, since it's a transform between them)."

becomes

" where the Bundle.identifier is equivalent to ClinicalDocument.id."

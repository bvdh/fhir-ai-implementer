- **Description**: In the discussion of the relationship between FHIR Documents and CDA documents, it is stated that:
Composition resources are wrapped into Document structures, for exchange of the whole package (the composition and its parts), and this wrapped, sealed entity is equivalent to a CDA document, where the Bundle.id is equivalent in function to ClinicalDocument.id (but it is not identical when interconverting, since it's a transform between them).
I think that it is Bundle.identifier that is equivalent to ClinicalDocument.id (since both are business identifiers).

### 3. Peter Jordan - 2021-01-31

The quote from the FHIR specification looks correct to me, i.e. Bundle.id is equivalent in function to ClinicalDocument.id. To say that Bundle.identifier is equivalent to ClinicalDocument.id looks incorrect to me - id and identifier are two different data types that serve two different purposes and, in particular, a resource id is definitely not a business identifier.

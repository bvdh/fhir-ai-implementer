- **Description**: The _content search parameter shall search across the DocumentReference.content.attachment.data, and DocumentReference.content.attachment.url.

When searching in Attachement.data, does this mean that the search is in the Base64 content or in the derialized content? Does it search in the raw data or does it need to understand the content as well (I hope not)?

Related to searching on the url, does this mean they search for text in the url or in the content of the URL? I assume the the server is not required to support searching in the content pointed to be the URL. That would be very expensive. Please clarify.

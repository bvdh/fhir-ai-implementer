- **Description**: It is useful to be able to query for reports on particular anatomy of a patient.

For modality, it is fairly query how to do a chained search through study (I think).
GET [base]/DiagnosticReport?study.modality=$DCM#CT
(Could still consider a search parameter on DiagnosticReport I suppose)

For body-structure, it's less clear. A body-structure query parameter on DiagnosticReport could include guidance on how to deal with the lack of a top-level study.body-structure (it's at the series level). Or if the chaining can be demonstrated to be tidy, could just be guidance in DiagnosticReport without a parameter.

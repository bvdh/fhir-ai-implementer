- **Description**: The AllergyIntolerance resource could benefit from a direct reference to the Procedure in which an allergic reaction was identified. A common example occurs during radiology procedures involving intravenous contrast administration, where adverse reactions may be observed and documented. An explicit relationship between the identified allergy and the originating procedure would facilitate traceability and could support automated inclusion of newly identified allergies in imaging reports. For example, an ImagingStudy/Procedure referenced from a DiagnosticReport could be linked to the corresponding AllergyIntolerance that was established as a result of that procedure.

One possible modeling approach would be to extend AllergyIntolerance.reaction.onset[x] beyond its current dateTime option to also support a reference to the triggering Procedure (or, more generally, another relevant clinical event). This would allow systems to capture not only when a reaction occurred but also the context in which it was observed and documented.

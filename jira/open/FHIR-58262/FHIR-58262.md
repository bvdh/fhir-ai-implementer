https://hl7.org/fhir/uv/ibcm/2024Sep/background.html#which-fhir-resource-do-we-use

Change the text from:

During a maternity encounter, the Patient and Encounter resources for the mother will be present. After the child is born, new Patient, Encounter and RelatedPerson (for the mother) records will be created, if required (unless a fetal record exists).

To

During a maternity encounter, the Patient and Encounter resources for the mother will be present. If a fetal record doesn't exist at the time of birth, a new Patient, Encounter and RelatedPerson (for the mother) records can be created.

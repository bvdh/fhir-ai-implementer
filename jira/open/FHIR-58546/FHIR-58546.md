- **Description**: The definitions of `ResearchStudy.focus` and `ResearchStudy.condition` overlap because both refer to the focus or concern of the study.

Please clarify their intended distinction. Presumably, `condition` represents the disease or health condition being studied, while `focus` represents the intervention, product, procedure, test, or other subject under investigation.

`ResearchStudy.focus` currently permits references to `PlanDefinition`, among other resources. Please consider also allowing references to:

`ActivityDefinition`, for a defined intervention or activity under study
`ObservationDefinition`, for a defined test or measurement being evaluated

Both resources can already be referenced by `PlanDefinition.action.definition[x]`. Guidance should also distinguish a `PlanDefinition` used as the study focus from one referenced through `ResearchStudy.protocol`.

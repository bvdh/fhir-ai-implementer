- **Description**: Summary: Widen CareTeam.managingOrganization so the entity responsible for a care team can be an individual, the patient, or a related person.

Element: CareTeam.managingOrganization (0..* Reference(Organization))

Description: CareTeam's scope explicitly includes family members, guardians and the patient, and the resource supports proxies (onBehalfOf), guardianship evidence (supportingInfo) and rosters (effective[x] Timing). But the only way to say who is responsible for the team is managingOrganization, which allows Organization only. Many care teams outside institutions are managed by an individual: a family member coordinating a care circle, a patient self-managing a disability or aged-care package, or an independent support worker/practitioner. These cannot currently be represented except by inference from a participant role. By comparison, Group.managingEntity in the same release allows Organization | Practitioner | PractitionerRole | RelatedPerson.

Proposed resolution:

Widen targets of CareTeam.managingOrganization to Organization | Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Patient | RelatedPerson.
Short: "Entity responsible for the care team". Definition: "The organization or individual responsible for the care team. For care teams coordinated outside an organization, this may be a practitioner, the patient, or a related person (e.g. a family member coordinating care)."
(Optional, WG to decide) Rename to managingEntity for consistency with Group.managingEntity. This is a breaking change and is not required to meet the need; widening the targets alone is sufficient.

Impact: Target widening is compatible, substantive. Rename (if chosen) is non-compatible.

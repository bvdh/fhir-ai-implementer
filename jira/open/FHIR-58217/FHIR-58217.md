The list is accurate and relevant as of the time it was created. It will not be revised. When accessed in the future, it will still be an accurate and relevant list of the state at time of creation but should not be considered current.

List.mode=snapshot seems to work, but no List.status values seem correct. Unless "current" means "current as of .date, which might be several years ago so not really current-current". In which case .date needs to be present to give "current" meaning.

Might be worth reviewing how .mode, .status, and .date are semantically partitioned in terms of answering questions like: Is there a chance the content is stale? How up-to-date is the information? Is the information a delta or a full list? Is this list being updated/managed by someone/something?

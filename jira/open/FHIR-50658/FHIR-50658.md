- **Description**: The Device Module would be a great place to give some guidance and big picture of how Procedures and ServiceRequests relate to Devices that are not patient specific but were used or requested to be used to provide a service. For example, Radiotherapy Treatment Delivery Systems are not patient specific, but for the clinical record it is important to capture with which treatment machine (Device) a patient was treated or is requested to be treated. E.g. as Procedure.performer.actor? Or in ServiceRequest as orderDetail.parameter? ServiceRequest.performer would also allow to add a Device reference, but then per comment in the ServiceRequest.performer element would be considered an alternative performer to any referenced human performer. As ServiceRequest and Procedure are developed by different working groups, it would be important to have a coordinated big picture how we reference treatment devices from these resources.

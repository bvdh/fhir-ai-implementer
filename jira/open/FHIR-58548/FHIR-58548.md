- **Description**: The distinction between `ResearchStudy.keyword` and `ResearchStudy.focus` is unclear. `focus` identifies what the study investigates, while `keyword` is only described as being used to search for or filter studies.

Using the same example ValueSet, `ResearchStudyFocusType`, for both elements further suggests that they have equivalent semantics. Please clarify whether `keyword` may describe any aspect of the study and whether information already represented in `focus`, `condition`, or other elements is expected to be repeated as a keyword. A dedicated example ValueSet may better illustrate the intended scope.

FHIR already uses `topic` for similar searchable metadata on resources such as `Library`, `PlanDefinition`, `ActivityDefinition`, and `Measure`, and an `artifact-topic` extension exists for other definitional artifacts. Please consider whether `ResearchStudy.keyword` should align with this established `topic` pattern or document why a separate `keyword` concept is needed.

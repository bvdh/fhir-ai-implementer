- **Description**: Given the role that AI can and may play in the delivery of healthcare, the narrative guidance and examples associated with the use of Device should be updated to include more robust coverage for the use of the Device Resource wrt AI Agents.

Clearly explain why responsible party information may be required and how to use the owner element to include the responsible party.

This change is requested in conjunction with a change that is also being requested for FHIR R6 where modeling has changed to move Device Ownership into a relationship expressed using the DeviceAssociation Resource. (FHIR-59387)

### 2. Stephen Chu - 2026-10-08

In some cases, post-operative diagnosis may agree with/confirm preoperative diagnosis, e.g. preoperatively radiological diagnosis of intertrochanteric fracture algins with diagnosis confirmed introperatively.

In some cases, pre-operative and intraoperative diagnosis will be refined by hisopath diagnosis post-operatively. Example: pre-operative radiological (e.g. MRI) diagnosis = "high grade glioma in right frontal lobe, ?oligodendroglioma"; intraoperative diagnosis may be established by color and texture, consistency/firmness of the tumour tissue, and intraoperative sampling (for real time histopath examination. But the definitive diagnosis, e.g. oligodendroglioma, IDHI mutated, WHO Grade 2.
In such cases, diagnosis established during the operation is likely to be refined / revised by histopath diagnosis.

Adding an "intraoperative diagnosis" extension may be a consideration.
The final diagnosis will be recorded using Condition resource.

### 3. Michael Padula - 2026-09-23

There are elements for Pre-operative Diagnosis and Post-operative Diagnosis from the Operative Note in c-CDA (and the associated logical model): http://hl7.org/cda/us/ccda/StructureDefinition/PostoperativeDiagnosisSection

Most commonly the post-op diagnosis matches the pre-op one, but there's an important rationale and precedent for capturing both of these fields.

Is there a best practice for representing these two fields in the Procedure resource?

Procedure.reason seems incomplete to represent both those fields.

Similarly, Procedure.supportingInfo may also work but seems non-specific.

Not sure how https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/CDA-ccda/StructureDefinition-PostoperativeDiagnosisSection.html ties back to Procedure.

- **Description**: ServiceRequest.status provides several distinct terminal states (completed, ended, entered-in-error, revoked), while operationally meaningful active states such as (wait-)listed, scheduled, and postponed appear to collapse into active. This creates lossy mappings for systems that distinguish these states explicitly.

- **Description**: In the context of healthcare technology management and clinical alarm systems, Advisory is typically the alert type, while Information represents a low alert priority level.

However, the Scope and Usage section currently states that priorities range from High (e.g., a life-threatening condition requiring immediate response) to Advisory. Advisory is not a priority value; it is a DeviceAlert.type. The defined priority values are high, medium, low, and info.

In addition, the opening sentence says the alert represents something that "DeviceAlert represents a single alert condition detected and signaled by a device to create -- awareness of a risk or occurrence that needs to be addressed.," while the info priority explicitly represents a condition that does not need addressing.

Please, revise Section 11.2.1 to clearly distinguish alert type from alert priority, and to accommodate informational/advisory conditions that may not require action.

Suggested wording:

DeviceAlert represents a single alert condition or advisory detected or generated by a device to create awareness of a risk, condition, or occurrence that may require attention or response.

Also, revise the priority paragraph to:

Alerts can have different priorities based on the severity of the condition and urgency of response, ranging from High to Information Only. Separately, DeviceAlert.type can distinguish physiological, technical, and advisory alerts.

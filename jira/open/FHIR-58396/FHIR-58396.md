- **Related Section(s)**: 14.4.4
- **Resolution Description**: We will add CareTeam as an allowed target of MedicationStatement.author, which becomes Reference(Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Organization | Patient | RelatedPerson | Device | CareTeam), 0..1 unchanged. We will update the definition to "The individual, organization, care team, or device that created the statement and has responsibility for its content."

Rationale: a medication statement can be compiled and maintained by a care team (for example, a reconciled medication list) rather than by one individual. CareTeam is already an allowed author or recorder in other resources that capture responsibility for content, such as Composition.author, DocumentReference.author and FamilyMemberHistory.recorder.

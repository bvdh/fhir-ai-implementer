Specific to the Group questions you asked, the history goes back to when GrahameGrieve logged several JIRAs for Group to be added to meet the use case in Africa where care is very often provided to and administered for families, not patients, and families are considered to act as a group.

The past discussions (broadly) about Group resulted in this being added to the Group resource (Group - FHIR v6.0.0-snapshot1)
NOTE: Group may be used to define families or households, which in some circumstances may act collectively or have a degree of legal or formal recognition. This should be considered an exception. When Group is used for types of entities other than Patient or RelatedPerson, the expectation remains that the Group will not act collectively or have formal recognition - use Organization if these behaviors are needed.

For example, it is possible for a 'family' Group to be a performer of an Observation or owner of a Task. However, this is not permitted for a Group made up of Practitioners, PractitionerRoles or Organizations. Organization or CareTeam would need to be used instead. A Group of Practitioners could, however, be a subject of an Observation.
A couple of the historical FHIR-I (Group) discussions were part of FHIR-47603, FHIR-49851, FHIR-50525 etc.

### 3. Lisa R. Nelson - 2026-09-17

Ron: this is a VERY IMPORTANT QUESTION, and one that should not be discussed in a small group without sufficient involvement of people what are really digging into the subject of AI. What roles a Device should be allowed to play is a MAJOR debate right now, and this seemingly simplistic question is at the heart of it all. We need to unpack the language a bit because many notions are lurking beneath the surface of these words. Recorder - I think that means Author. Asserter, is that another word for Attester? Is it a synonym for authenticator? We need to clearly understand what role we are talking about and what that role implies. Next we need to ask about Responsibility and Accountability. Who can be responsible for doing this Asserter role? Is it appropriate for that role to be assigned to/performed by a Device? Or is this a situation where a person is the performer and the Device is just a tool that employed by that person to perform the duties which the person is responsible for performing? Next question: Who is Accountable? Is it implied that the recorder is responsible for documenting something, and the asserter is accountable for that something which is recorded is right? Can a Device be accountable? Or can only people and the organizations they work for be accountable? I hope you can see the gravity of this question and appreciate why it needs to be discussed at high levels and in big circles.

Can a group be responsible for recording something? What would that look like? Can a Group be accountable for the accuracy and quality of the something that gets recorded? What does shared responsibility look like? Who performs how much of the work or which part of the work? What does Group accountability look like? We took a vote, and it passed, now we are all in this together? Is each party at risk for a portion of liability, or is the accountability shared in some other way? I think there are big questions about Group as recorder and Group as asserter/attester/authenticator? But they seem less pressing than the AI-driven focus on Devices.

Rongparker if you are going to be discussing this at the Working Group in September, I'd love to participate. It almost seems like it could be it's own Birds of a Feather topic, but i don't think there are any good BOF spots left. Maybe just Q6 in the lobby bar! Or standing on a box in Hyde park? Questions like these deserve serious debate and broad involvement.

- **Resolution Description**: Add RelatedPerson to type choices for ArtifactAssessment.content.author
- **Description**: ArtifactAssessment.content.author is defined as

> Indicates who or what authored the content.

The target types are allowed to be: Patient | Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Organization | Device)

The target types should allow for RelatedPerson - if a Patient can do it, so can the patient's carer.

- **Resolution Description**: Change the short description to be 'CareTeam instance identifier'.

Change the short descriptions for item.detail.sequence to 'Item detail instance identifier'.

Change the short descriptions for item.detail.subDetail.sequence to 'Item subDetail instance identifier'.

Change the description of the CareTeam.sequence to be 'a unique identifier for the suite of properties' on the CareTeam instance so that the instance may be uniquely referenced within and outside of the resource.

The similar change shall also be applied to the sequence element in diagnosis, supportingInfo, procedure, insurance, item, item.detail, item.detail.subDetail.

- **Applied for Version**: R6
- **Description**: The note on this element says:

> Necessary to maintain the order of the care team and provide a mechanism to link individuals to claim details.

why is it necessary? Why not just use the order in the instance? If there's a reason for this to actually exist, then it should be explained.

- **Description**: AdverseEvent.subject 1..1 Reference(Patient | Group | Practitioner | RelatedPerson)

It is possible that an entire CareTeam can be impacted by an AdverseEvent.
Case example: care team using Radiation Personal Protective Equipment (RPPE) such as lead aprons that were contaminated by COVID virus during an OR procedure.

It is also possible that an entire department | room | organization be contaminated by radioactive pharmaceuticals through accidental leakage.

Suggest consider inclusion of CareTeam and Organization in the AdverseEvent.subject 1..1 Reference list

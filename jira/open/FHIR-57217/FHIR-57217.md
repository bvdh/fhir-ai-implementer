- **Description**: The patient compartment often incorporates resources using a patient search parameter. For many resource types (e.g., CarePlan, Encounter, Condition), the patient search parameter is defined something like this: CareTeam.subject.where(resolve() is Patient)

However, the .subject property that is the basis for the patient search parameter allows Groups, where those Groups can include patients. Example: a couples therapy Encounter could have an Encounter.subject -> Group, where the Group contains each partner (Patient) in the couple. So Encounter.subject -> Group -> [Patient].

Per the definition of the patient compartment, that group therapy Encounter would not be in the patient compartment.

Thus in general, the patient compartment may often be missing a significant amount of clinical resources that are relevant to that patient care - it would be missing all resources that link to the patient via a Group. This includes nested linking. E.g., Encounter -> Group -> (sub)Group -> (sub)Group -> Patient. I haven't dug into whether there are other indirect patient relationships that go through resources other than Group.

Implementer Note: we (Epic) don't use patient compartment at all in our implementation. Instead, we use our own internal categorization to identify all data about a patient.

- **Description**: The scope of RequestOrchestration is

> The RequestOrchestration resource is used to represent a set of optional and related activities that may be performed for a specific patient or context.

There's nothing about that definition that confines its use to institutional care. But the only kind of resources that can author a RequestOrchestration are Device | Practitioner | PractitionerRole. Why can't a patient or their designation carer author a RequestOrchestration in a social/home/family care situation?

Please add Patient and RelatedPerson to the author element

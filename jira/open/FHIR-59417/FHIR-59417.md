# FHIR-59417: EpisodeOfCare: don't require an organization; allow self/family care coordination

## Metadata

- **Summary**: EpisodeOfCare: don't require an organization; allow self/family care coordination
- **Created**: 2026-09-23
- **Updated**: 2026-10-13
- **Status**: Triaged
- **Project**: FHIR Specification Feedback
- **Type**: Change Request
- **Reporter**: Grahame Grieve
- **Resolution**: Unresolved
- **Specification**: FHIR Core (FHIR)
- **Raised in Version**: R6
- **Work Group**: Patient Administration
- **Related Artifact(s)**: EpisodeOfCare
- **Description**: Summary: EpisodeOfCare definitions assume an organization and practitioner-only coordination; adjust wording and widen careManager so the resource suits self-managed, family-coordinated and independent-provider care.

Elements: EpisodeOfCare (resource definition, short), introduction, managingOrganization, careManager, careTeam, reason, reason.value

Description: EpisodeOfCare is defined as "an association between a patient and an organization / healthcare provider(s)", and the introduction says the minimum is "a patient/group, organization and a reason". But managingOrganization is 0..1, and episodes in community and social care are often coordinated by an individual provider (e.g. a self-employed support worker under a consumer-directed funding scheme), by the patient, or by family. careManager allows only Practitioner | PractitionerRole. Several short descriptions ("Other practitioners…", "medical reasons") also discourage use for social care.

Proposed resolution:

Resource short: "An association of a Patient or Group with a care provider for a period of time during which the provider assumes some level of responsibility". Definition: "An association between a patient/group and a care provider (typically an organization, but possibly an individual) during which time encounters may occur. The provider assumes a level of responsibility for the patient during this time."
Introduction: change "a patient/group, organization and a reason" to "a patient/group, the responsible provider (usually an organization) and a reason".
managingOrganization: add comment "May be absent where the episode is coordinated by an individual provider, or by the patient or their family."
careManager: widen targets to Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Patient | RelatedPerson. Definition: "The person who is the care manager/care coordinator for this patient. Where care is self-managed or family-coordinated this may be the patient or a related person."
careTeam short: "Care team(s) participating in this episode of care".
reason short: "The list of reasons that are expected to be addressed during the episode of care"; reason.value short: "Reason to be addressed" (health or social).

Impact: careManager target widening is compatible, substantive; the rest are non-substantive. (Encounter.reason short "medical reasons" could be aligned at the same time.)

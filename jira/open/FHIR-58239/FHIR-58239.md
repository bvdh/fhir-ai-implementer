- **Resolution Description**: In the definition (Long Description) of DeviceAlert.acknowledged, add at the end: "The acknowledgement of the signal(s) related to the alert condition does not imply acknowledgement of the alert condition"
In the definition (Long Description) of DeviceAlert.signal.presence, add at the end: "The acknowledgement of the alert condition does not imply acknowledgement of the related signal(s)"
In Section 11.2.1.3 make the following change:
A signal associated with a DeviceAlert begins when the device initiates annunciation of an active alert condition, represented by the creation of signal element with a unique signal.identifier, signal.activationState = on and signal.presence = on. The signal may evolve through multiple states, such as being paused (signal.activationState = paused) when temporarily suppressed, acknowledged (signal.presence = ack) when user action stops annunciation while the condition may persist, or modified (e.g., annunciator changes). Note that acknowledgement of the condition is generally independent of the acknowledgement of related signals. Each state change can be captured as a distinct signal element with the same signal.identifier. When the alert condition ends (occurrence.end is set), the signal typically transitions to off, but may instead enter a latched state (signal.presence = latched) where annunciation persists beyond the condition to ensure awareness, before finally stopping. Throughout its lifecycle, the signal is thus a dynamic, time-segmented representation of how the device communicates the alert condition, independent of but driven by the underlying condition lifecycle.

Issue:
The resource has a condition-level acknowledged boolean indicating whether the alert condition has been acknowledged. Separately, signal.presence = ack means that signal annunciation has stopped because a user acknowledged the signal.

The Background section similarly describes signal.presence = ack as a user action that stops annunciation while the underlying condition may persist.

The specification does not clearly state whether:

signal.presence = ack

implies:

DeviceAlert.acknowledged = true

or whether these represent intentionally independent concepts.

That ambiguity could lead different implementations to produce different combinations for the same device behavior.

Proposed change:

Add guidance explaining the semantic relationship between alert-condition acknowledgment and signal acknowledgment. If signal.presence = ack normally implies that the alert condition has been acknowledged, state that relationship. If the two concepts are intentionally independent, provide an example explaining when one may be acknowledged without the other.

### 2. Michael Faughn - 2026-08-26

DeviceAlert.acknowledged refers to an acknowledgment of the condition that the Alert is about.

'DeviceAlert.signal.presence = ack' refers to acknowledgement of the signal (e.g., the clinician heard it or a mobile device has received it (perhaps clarified by a profile)). The 'ack' value means that awareness of the signal has been recorded and that the signal is now off.

This resource does not imply any coupling between the 'acknowledged' and 'signal.presence = ack'.

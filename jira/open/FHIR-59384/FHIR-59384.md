- **Description**: Consent.manager is defined as:

> The actor that manages the consent through its lifecycle.

The allowed resource types for the target are: HealthcareService | Organization | Patient | Practitioner

But not RelatedPerson - why? If a patient can manage a consent, so can their delegated agent, a family member. This is more pressing when people use FHIR in a social care setting, rather than in an institutional setting.

Please add RelatedPerson as an allowed target type.

The same logic applies to Consent.controller: If a patient can do it, so can their carer.

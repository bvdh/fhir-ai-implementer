- **Resolution Description**: Revisions to the 10.1.2 BNR text for Observation:

An Observation resource can be used to capture point in time observations of the presence, likely presence, absence, or likely absence of a condition. Such Observations are not updated as the condition changes or resolves, either with or without treatment (such new information could be captured in a new Observation instance with the corresponding point in time). The Condition Observation resource is should not be used to record a clinical condition or diagnosis about a patient or subject that must have a clinical status which can be updated over time, perhaps to track resolution, progress or the response to treatmentare typically captured in the Condition resource or the ClinicalAssessment resource.

The Observation resource is often referenced by the Condition.evidence may reference Observations resource to provide specific subjective and objective data to support its assertions, including symptoms and assessments of the presence or absence of diagnostically significant phenomena. Symptoms may sometimes be recorded as Conditions if the no etiology is being explored has yet been identified, or if the symptom is severe enough to merit independent treatment, but in most cases a symptom can be represented in an Observation with a relationship to the Condition of concern.

...

A DiagnosticReport references +Observation resources to provide the atomic results. In contrast to the Observation resource, tThe DiagnosticReport resource typically includes additional clinical context and some mix of atomic results, images, imaging reports, textual and coded interpretation, and formatted representations. Laboratory reports, pathology reports, and imaging reports should be represented using the DiagnosticReport resource. The Observation resource is referenced by the DiagnosticReport to provide the atomic results for a particular investigation. The Observation resource may also be used to represent impressions or conclusions in DiagnosticReport resources. Laboratories routinely have a variable that is summative across a series of discrete variables - these are usually called 'impressions' or 'interpretations'. Sometimes they are algorithmically specified and sometimes they have the imprimatur of pathologists and they are conveyed in Observation or DiagnosticReport resources instead of the ClinicalImpression{+}Assessment resource. The Observation resource should not be used to record clinical diagnosis about a patient or subject as discussed above.

Similar to the DiagnosticReport resource, ClinicalImpression captures a point-in-time interpretation of a collection of data by a clinician with similar references to Observation resources.

Need to clarify the distinction that Observation resources capture point-in-time "statements" of the presence or absence of a condition, while Condition resources are created to have an updateable "current" status of a condition, generally to facilitate tracking and/or treatment, perhaps as part of a problem list.

- **Resolution Description**: We will revert the effect of FHIR-54014. DosageDetails.renderedInstruction is 0..1 (it was changed back to 0..1 on 2026-09-08 as a build fix; this resolution confirms it), and the requirement wording is removed from the definition:

Definition: "The full representation of the dosage instructions, as text for display to and use by humans."
Comment: "renderedInstruction SHOULD be present when the dosage is intended to be read and acted on by people, such as prescribers, pharmacists, those administering the medication, or the patient. It can be omitted when the exchange does not need a human-readable rendering of the dosage, such as for logistics or supply where the dosage is not acted upon. When present, it SHALL be consistent with the structured dosage, and may contain additional information not found in the structured content. See Dosage text and structured data."
- **Resolution Vote**: José Costa Teixeira / Frank McKinney : 6-0-0
- **Vote Date**: 2026-10-19
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: Revert FHIR-54014.
Dosage instructions should be optional. Making it mandatory would require changes in all systems that do not implement or need rendered dosages.
There are systems that just have to "deliver" the meds, not worried about the human-readable instructions.
This would be a change to implementers and should be optional - at most a best practice recommendation.
See https://chat.fhir.org/#narrow/channel/179165-committers/topic/DosageDetails.2ErenderedInstruction/with/622378722

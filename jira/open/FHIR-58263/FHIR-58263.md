- **Specification**: FHIR Core (FHIR)

- **Work Group**: Pharmacy
- **Outstanding Negatives**: Normative
- **Related Page(s)**: Dosage
- **Resolution Description**: Add to the comment on Dosage.additionalInstruction: "Coded supplemental instructions or warnings, such as 'with food' or 'may cause drowsiness', that systems can process, check or translate. Free-text instructions in terms the patient understands are conveyed in patientInstruction. The same instruction can be conveyed in both."
Add a comment to Dosage.patientInstruction: "Free-text instructions in terms the patient or consumer understands, intended to be conveyed to them as written. This can restate content that is also coded in additionalInstruction."
- **Description**: The distinction between Dosage.additionalInstruction and Dosage.patientInstruction is difficult to apply consistently.

Statements such as “take with food”, “take with plenty of water”, “do not take with alcohol”, or “may cause drowsiness” could reasonably fit both elements.

Is the intended distinction that additionalInstruction represents discrete/codable dosage instructions or warnings, while patientInstruction contains free-text guidance intended to be conveyed to the patient as-is?

Please clarify the intended usage of both elements and provide guidance on when to use additionalInstruction, patientInstruction, or both.

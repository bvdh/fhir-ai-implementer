- **Description**: The resource definition still describes DeviceAssociation as recording an association or dissociation of a device with a subject and/or operator. However, Ballot 5 no longer has an operation or operator element. The new model uses required subject and optional focus; the Ballot Note explicitly states that the old operation backbone was collapsed into relationship/focus/subject.

The same outdated wording remains in the detailed resource definition as below.

Element Id
DeviceAssociation

Definition
A record of association or dissociation of a device with a subject (e.g. patient) and/or operator.

Proposed change:

"A record of an association or dissociation between a device and one or more associated focus entities."

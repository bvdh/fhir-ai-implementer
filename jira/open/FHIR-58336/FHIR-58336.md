- **Description**: On the Mappings to Other standards page and the relevant resource mapping tabs the statement "The HL7 v2-FHIR Implementation guide, located here , provides additional guidance on transforming an HL7 v2 message to FHIR resources for FHIR R4 and FHIR R6."

Suggest to replace the word "additional" with "comprehensive" or similar word to further clarify that the mappings on the resources are summary and that the v2-FHIR guide can be updated more frequently as we continue to expand its content to cover all of v2.

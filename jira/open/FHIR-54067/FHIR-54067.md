- **Description**: Current text:

The Signature SHALL include a "srCms signer commitments" property for the Purpose(s) of Signature. The Purpose can be the action being attested to, or the role associated with the signature. The Signature.type shall come from ASTM E1762-95(2013). The Signature.type SHALL contain the same values as the srCms element.

Comment - twofold:

1) It'd probably be better to update "The Signature.type shall come from ASTM E1762-95(2013)". Currently, the binding type in signature.type in the datatypes page is preferred, plus it contains non-ASTM codes

2) Can we get some examples of what srCms having the same values as signature.type looks like? (I had some internal discussions with colleagues on this as the structure of srCms is:

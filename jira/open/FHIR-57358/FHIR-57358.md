The following clarifications/editorial improvements are suggested for the BNR text of the Observation Resource:

...

At its core, the Observation name-value pair structures are open-ended; Observation allows expressing a name-value pair or structured collection of name-value pairs. As such, it can support conveying any type of information desired. Hhowever, itthat is not its intent. Observation is intended for capturing measurements and subjective point-in-time assessments,. It is not intended to be used for those specific contexts and use cases already covered by other FHIR resources. For example, the use AllergyIntolerance forresource represents a patient allergies, MedicationStatement resourcefor: medications taken by a patient, FamilyMemberHistory forresource: a patient's family history, Procedure forresource: information about a procedure, and QuestionnaireResponse forresource: a set of answers to a set of questions.

...

There will however be situations of overlap. For example, a response to a question of "have you ever taken illicit drugs" could in principle may be represented by some systems using MedicationStatement, but othermost systems would treat such an assertion as an Observation.
In some cases, such as when source data is coming from an HL7 V2 feed, a system might not have information that allows it to distinguish diagnosis, allergy and other "specialized" types of observations from laboratory, vital sign and other observation types intended to be conveyed with this resource. In those circumstances, such specialized observations that would use another resource may also appear using this resource. Adhering to such convention is an appropriate use of Observation.

...

The Observation resource is used extensively for observations about people, groups, devices, locations, substances, and procedures. It does not encode events triggered by the Observation. – not about the record describing these entities. For example, DeviceAlert represents a single alert or alarm condition detected and signaled by a patient-connected health / medical device to create clinician’s awareness of a patient safety risk that needs to be addressed, whereas Observation captures any evidentiary data needed to interpret the alert condition or that is the reason why the alert condition is present.

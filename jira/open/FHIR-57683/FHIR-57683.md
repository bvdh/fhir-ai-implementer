- **Description**: Currently, the only way to record a location as the value of an observation is by lat/long coordinates or in a text form.

To support observations of the location of a device (or location of patient, etc.), and potentially other use cases, allow:

valueReference(Location)
component.valueReference(Location)
Update/create searchParameters appropriately
Update ObservationDefinition as needed to support definition of such observations

#implementers > Observation value of Location?

We should further if other resources could be the value of an observation, especially if we recall that Observation has a wide scope beyond patient.

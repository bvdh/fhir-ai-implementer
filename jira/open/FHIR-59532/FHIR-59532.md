- **Description**: 1.
MeasureReport.group.population.count and MeasureReport.group.population.countQuantity are both 0..1 and both have a rule mrp-3 "The count and countQuantity elements SHALL be mutually exclusive"

But the expression says something differently: "count.exists() xor countQuantity.exists()"

Which is different than what the text says because exists xor exists means that one and only one of them exists and doesn't give the option of neither existing. If the expression is the intention then the description text of the rule needs to be updated to say something like "One and only one of either count or countQuantity must exist." To say we don't give the option of none for both. But I don't think that's the case so then the expression needs to be "count.empty() or countQuantity.empty()"

Also MeasureReport.group.population has the mrp-3 rule as well, which is where it's defined.

2.
There are also the elements for MeasureReport.group.stratifier.stratum.population ** has the mrp-4 rule that seems to have a similar problem too, which can also be fixed with "count.empty() or countQuantity.empty()"

3.
In the test files: mrp-3.f1.fail.xml and mrp-4.f1.fail.xml both misspell countQuantity as countQuanity.

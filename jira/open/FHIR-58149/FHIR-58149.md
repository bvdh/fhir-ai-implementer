- **Resolution Description**: We will make the following changes:

New Observation.device definition: Identifies the device primarily responsible for generating the observation or measurement.

Add to existing Observation.supportingDevice definition: "Observation.supportingDevice identifies other devices used in generating the observation that contributed to the process but were not primarily responsible for producing the result."

And this would be a comment on supportingDevice:

This element supports identifying devices involved in producing a particular observation without implying that they generated the measurement. Devices that are components of, or have an established relationship with, the primary measurement device may instead be represented through Device relationships. Use supportingDevice when the association is specific to the observation rather than an inherent relationship between devices.
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Applied for Version**: R6
- **Description**: IMHO this newly added element does not meet the 80/20 rule for being a first class element of Observation:

a supportingDevice element could just as well be added to the device resource associated with the device generating the observation
compelling examples are missing
a parent/child device combination can be used as well in many cases

It would be more appropriate to model this as an extension, either in an IG oir in the core extension pack.

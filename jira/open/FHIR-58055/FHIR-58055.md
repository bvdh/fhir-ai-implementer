- **Description**: In AdverseEvent "scope and usage" paragraph, it is written "These events are characterized by the need to capture cause-and-effect (although they might not be known at the time of the event), severity, and outcome.". However, there is no severity field since R5.

I suggest to add a severity field and to align field name / binding / description with Condition.severity

https://build.fhir.org/condition.html

# FHIR-59394: MedicationRequest.recorder is restricted to a Practitioner | PractitionerRole

- **Description**: MedicationRequest.recorder is restricted to a Practitioner | PractitionerRole. A medication can be requested by a Patient or their carer, but only recorded by a practitioner. This is appropriate for inpatient care, but it's not obvious to me that this is an appropriate restriction for all contexts of care, including home/family/social care settings.

I think that if a patient or their carer can request a medication, then they should be able to record that they have. there's nothing in the definition of recorder that says that it can't be a patient or carer:

> The person who entered the order on behalf of another individual for example in the case of a verbal or a telephone order.

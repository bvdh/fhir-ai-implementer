- **Description**: 1. The W3C Health Care and Life Sciences (HCLS) Interest Group https://www.w3.org/blog/hcls/ linked at the top of the page should be renamed to the HCLS Community Group https://www.w3.org/community/hclscg/ which has continued maintenance & development.

2. The examples on this page should be made easier to read by at least highlighting the inline comments. Could be done with new CSS class or simply reuse <span class="xmlcomment"> just to change the color.

3. Update formatted "FHIR allows modifierExtensions" to "modifier extensions" and link directly to extensibility.html#modifierExtension .

4. Update direct links to http://build.fhir.org/fhir.shex and http://build.fhir.org/fhir.ttl instead of the zip file https://build.fhir.org/fhir.rdf.ttl.zip for better accessibility

5. Clarify that mappings to HL7v3 are incomplete and update link to HL7v3 from https://dev3.hl7.org/implement/standards/product_brief.cfm?product_id=77 to https://www.hl7.org/implement/standards/product_brief.cfm?product_id=77

6. Update full set of RDF specific terms as fhir:nodeRole, fhir:treeRoot, fhir:v, and fhir:l listed at https://build.fhir.org/rdf.html#ontologies

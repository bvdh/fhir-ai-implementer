- **Description**: ResearchStudy.status is defined as "The publication state of the resource (not of the study)." It is unclear whose publication context is meant: the publisher or maintainer of the FHIR resource, the source trial registry, or the receiving FHIR system.

Please clarify what draft, active, retired, and unknown mean for a ResearchStudy instance and explicitly distinguish these values from the operational or recruitment status of the study, which appears to belong in ResearchStudy.progressStatus.

The BRIDG mapping of ResearchStudy.status to StudyOverallStatus.code also appears inconsistent with "not of the study" and should be reviewed.

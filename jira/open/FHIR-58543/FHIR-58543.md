Current wording includes:

"This not where the item's ingredient are primarily stated..."

The definition has two grammatical errors: missing "is" and singular "ingredient" where plural is more appropriate.

Suggested change:

"This is not where the item's ingredients are primarily stated (for which see Ingredient.for or ManufacturedItemDefinition.ingredient)."

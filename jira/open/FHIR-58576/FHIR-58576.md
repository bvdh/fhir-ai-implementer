- **Description**: Group.combinationMethod's long description:

Used to specify how two or more characteristics are combined.

'all-of': Each of the characteristics must be met. This is functionally equivalent to combining all characteristics with an AND operator.
'any-of': At least one of the characteristics must be met. This is functionally equivalent to combining all characteristics with an OR operator.
'at-least': At least n of the characteristics must be met. Use Group.combinationThreshold to specify the value of n.
'at-most': At most n of the characteristics must be met. Use Group.combinationThreshold to specify the value of n.
'except-subset': The characteristics expressed as exclusion criteria are used as exceptions to meeting the characteristics expressed as inclusion criteria.

I suggest we add invariants, especially the suggested grp-3.

grp-3: It sounds like we could use an invariant for at-least and at-most

combinationThreshold.exists() = combinationMethod.exists($this = 'at-least' or $this = 'at-most')

grp-4: We could also have: combinationThreshold.empty() or combinationThreshold <= characteristic.count()

grp-5: And less importantly, we could have this as a warning since it's meaningless to have the combinationMethod if there are no characteristics: combinationMethod.exists() implies characteristic.count() > 1

- **Description**: Several questions about the use of MedicationDispense in a community context, and emergency contexts, where the patient might might not be known when dispensing.

MedicationDispense.subject is 1..1 Patient|Group. A counter sale to an unidentified customer still needs a Patient resource. So does "something for my child's fever", where the buyer isn't the patient and the patient is barely known. Either make subject 0..1 or document a minimal or contained Patient pattern. At the moment implementers will invent their own.
MedicationDispense has no reason element, and no link to a protocol. When there's no prescriber, the dispenser's clinical justification is the key record, for example a positive malaria rapid test under test-and-treat, or a symptom. MedicationRequest has reason but Dispense doesn't, so it ends up in supportingInformation or a note. basedOn only allows CarePlan, so a dispense can't point at the protocol that authorised it, such as a national standing order for community health workers or a treatment guideline in a PlanDefinition. we could add reason, and let basedOn reference PlanDefinition, or add instantiates.?
The category value sets are institution-relative. Both dispense and request categories are inpatient | outpatient | community | discharge. There's no code for non-prescription or over-the-counter supply, public health programme supply (HIV, TB, malaria), emergency use, marine use, or mass distribution. The bindings are example strength, but they're the default everyone copies.

I think it would be good for pharmacy to have a look at this flow, and be explicit at least about the intent

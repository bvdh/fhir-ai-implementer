- **Description**: `DocumentReference` provides dedicated elements for `author`, `attester` and `custodian`, but no general structure for other parties associated with the document, such as editors, reviewers, publishers, contributors or rights holders.

The structure of `ResearchStudy.associatedParty`, including `name`, `role`, `period`, `classifier` and `party`, could provide a model for representing these additional roles.

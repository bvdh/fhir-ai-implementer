- **Description**: Both Specimen and SpecimenDefinition have container devices as well as separate additive elements. Since the container may also specify additives, it is unclear if the additives are in addition to those already present in the container or merely restatements of those already present.

Similarly Device has an additive element, as well as a DeviceDefinition. Are the additives in Device additional to those in the definition? Device may be used in the absence of a Definition; how should additive be interpreted in that case?

(This should be reviewed by both OO HCP and Specimen groups.)

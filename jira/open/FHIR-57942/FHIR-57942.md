- **Description**: If a complete Study or Series is referenced by the ImagingSelection resource it makes sense to have the .endpoint directly under the ImagingSelection. However the cardianality for .instance is 0..*. In cases where multiple instance (e.g. multiple slices of a CT each encoded as CT Image Storage SOP instance) are included, there is no direct endpoint provided for them. Should enpoint also be included in the instances?

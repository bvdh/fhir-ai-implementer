- **Description**: RDF Turtle (.ttl) has a canonicalization that is standardized as a W3C recommendation: https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf-canon/

We can add a canonicalization for FHIR RDF Turtle, based on this standard, for the purpose of supporting digital signatures. This could go on the digital signatures page or at least the RDF page.

The canonicalization method could be identified by something like: http://hl7.org/fhir/canonicalization/rdf

The `Signature.targetFormat` could be specified as something like "text/turtle;canonicalization=http://hl7.org/fhir/canonicalization/rdf"

More details related to formatting and hashing can be added.

FHIR RDF Turtle is maintained by the ITS working group.

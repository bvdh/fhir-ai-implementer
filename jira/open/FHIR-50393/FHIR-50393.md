- **Description**: introduce an information model that helps to understand that

a panel is comprised of other vital sign measurements
a BMI uses two other profiles
we have one- and two-value profiles, eg body weight and blood pressure
...

Perhaps it is also necessary to introduce a separation that explains that not all measurements of a certain kind, eg blood pressure, are a vital sign value. Currently, many discussions lack a clarification in that regard.

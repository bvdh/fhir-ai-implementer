- **Description**: RegulatedAuthorization.status is defined as the regulatory status of the authorization, with "approved" given as an example. It is also marked as a modifier because the status can indicate that the record should not be treated as valid.

However, the element is bound Preferred to PublicationStatus. PublicationStatus is explicitly defined as "The lifecycle status of an artifact" and contains only:

draft
active
retired
unknown

with definitions describing whether a resource/artifact is ready for normal use.

These semantics do not clearly represent regulatory authorization states such as approved, suspended, revoked, withdrawn, expired, etc.

Suggested change:

Please review whether PublicationStatus is the appropriate preferred binding for RegulatedAuthorization.status. The element represents the regulatory status of the authorization, while PublicationStatus represents the lifecycle state of a FHIR artifact/resource. Consider defining or binding to a regulatory authorization status value set, or clarify how regulatory authorization states are expected to map to PublicationStatus.

# FHIR-59415: Encounter: support for home and community visit workflows

- **Description**: Summary: Encounter status handling assumes facility-based care; add statusReason, a community businessStatus workflow, fix the discharged definition, and clarify subjectStatus for home visits.

Elements: Encounter.status (encounter-status code system), Encounter.businessStatus, Encounter.subjectStatus; new Encounter.statusReason

Description: Encounter is increasingly used for home visits, community nursing, social care contacts and community health worker outreach. The required status codes are adequate, but the surrounding machinery assumes the patient comes to a facility:

There is no Encounter.statusReason. "No access" (no answer at the door, subject not home, refused at the door) is one of the most common outcomes of a home visit and must currently be recorded as cancelled/discontinued with the reason in a note. Most other event resources have a status reason.
businessStatus.type is inpatient | outpatient | emergency, and code sets are only provided for those workflows. Home/community visits have their own well-known workflow (allocated, en route, on site, no access, left site).
The definition of discharged includes "collating all required documentation and charge information before being released for billing". This is a hospital billing state; the underlying concept (care delivered, record not yet closed) applies in all settings.
subjectStatus codes (arrived | triaged | receiving-care | on-leave | departed, THO) assume the subject travels to the service. In a home visit the provider travels and the subject does not move.

Proposed resolution:

Add Encounter.statusReason 0..1 CodeableConcept, after status. Short: "Reason for current status". Definition: "Captures the reason for the current status of the encounter, particularly when the encounter was cancelled, discontinued or placed on hold." Example binding to a new value set encounter-status-reason (core code system), initial codes:

no-access — The provider was unable to gain access to the subject at the planned location (e.g. no answer).
subject-not-present — The subject was not at the planned location.
subject-declined — The subject (or their representative) declined the encounter at the time.
subject-unwell — The subject was too unwell for the encounter to proceed.
provider-unavailable — The provider was unable to attend or continue.
unsafe-environment — The encounter could not proceed safely at the location.

Add community to the encounter-businessstatus-type code system ("The business status is tracking a home or community visit workflow"), and add code system/value set encounter-businessstatus-community:

allocated — The visit has been allocated to a provider.
en-route — The provider is travelling to the visit location.
on-site — The provider has arrived at the visit location.
no-access — The provider attended but could not gain access to the subject.
left-site — The provider has left the visit location.

Redefine discharged (code unchanged): "The services of the Encounter have been delivered and the subject is no longer receiving care as part of it, but the Encounter record is not yet closed (e.g. documentation or other administrative activities remain). For inpatient encounters this corresponds to the subject having been discharged from the facility. The status moves to completed when the record is closed."
Add a comment to Encounter.subjectStatus: "For encounters where care is delivered at the subject's own location (e.g. home visits, outreach), the subject does not arrive or depart; subjectStatus may be omitted, or receiving-care used while care is being delivered. Provider-side progress for such encounters can be tracked using businessStatus." (Follow-up UTG request may add a not-present code to encounter-subject-status.)

Impact: Compatible, substantive (new element, new codes); definition change to discharged is non-substantive in intent.

- **Resolution Description**: In response to the commenter, category is not intended to overlap or replace information captured in other elements of the resource.

Resolution: Add comments text for .category clarifying that the element is to be used as a generic mechanism for categorizing the administration and is not fixed to a particular aspect or characteristic.

We will update MedicationAdministration.category as follows. No change to cardinality (0..*), type or binding.

Short: Grouping or category of the medication administration
Definition: A categorization of the medication administration, used for grouping, filtering and display. For example, it can indicate the care setting in which the administration takes place, such as inpatient, outpatient or community.
Comment: Category does not replace the more specific elements of the administration: the medication is in medication, the route, site and method are in dosage, and the reason is in reason. The example value set categorizes administrations by care setting; other categorizations, such as a local or regulatory classification of the medication, can also be used, and an administration can have categories from more than one categorization scheme.
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Description**: The definition of MedicationAdministration.category as "Type of medication administration" is unclear. It is not obvious what aspect of the administration should be represented by this element, especially since route, method, reason and other aspects are represented elsewhere.

Could the specification provide a more specific definition and/or an example showing when category should be used?

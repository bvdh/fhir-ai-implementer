## Comments

### 1. Cooper Thompson - 2026-09-16

To provide one data point, in Epic, we do not track reactions or manifestations for Immunizations directly on the Immunization itself. If a patient has a reaction to an immunization, that just goes in as an Allergy (not directly linked to the Immunization), and likely in a note.

We never populate Immunization.reaction at all.

Note that we do track more on the AllergyIntolerance side, including drug ingredient details, which can be "about" immunizations (but don't use the Immunization resource). See my comment on FHIR-59233 for what we do there.

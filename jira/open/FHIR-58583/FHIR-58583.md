- **Description**: For Group.combinationMethod, we could add a comment explaining what's useful and different about the "except-subset" code compared to just using the "all-of" code. The comment could explain that the "except-subset" tells that the "exclude = true" characteristics are specifically about what's in the inclusion characteristics.

For example, if we want a cohort defined by a Group resource that is about people who have breast cancer that isn't basal cancer, that would be exclude false for the characteristic breast cancer, and the second characteristic would be exclude true for basal cancer. If instead of "except-subset" we used "all-of" then if someone had cancer somewhere else on their body that was basal cancer then they would be excluded from the cohort. But with "except-subset" we are saying the exclude is specifically a property about the breast cancer. Of course we can come up with a better example.

Another example: "History of Agoraphobia EXCEPT during 2020-2021" without the "except-subset" the "2020-2021" is meaningless. (although the year range might be a little complicated for FHIR Group.characteristic)

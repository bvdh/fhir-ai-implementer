- **Description**: The Dosage examples have no titration: a dose increased in steps until a target or the highest tolerated dose is reached. Titration is common (for example pregabalin, gabapentin, antidepressants, antihypertensives; and tapering down, for example corticosteroids), and it exercises several parts of DosageInstruction: sequential steps, steps of variable length, times of day, and conditions on the increases.

Use case – a pharmacy label for pregabalin 25 mg capsules (120 capsules):

Day ONE to THREE: take ONE capsule at ONCE a day (at BEDTIME). Day FOUR to EIGHT: take ONE capsule each MORNING and ONE capsule at BEDTIME and then increase every THREE to SEVEN days to TWO capsules TWICE a day and THREE capsules TWICE a day, as directed. Caution: May cause drowsiness, dizziness; do not drive or operate …

The product information gives the criterion for the increases: "Based on individual patient response and tolerability, the dose may be increased … after an interval of 3 to 7 days". If an increase is not tolerated, the patient continues on the previous (last tolerated) dose.

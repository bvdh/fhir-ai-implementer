- **Resolution Description**: Will change the terminology binding on Composition.participant.type to be EXAMPLE instead of PREFERRED and change the description to reference "the document" instead of "the clinical document".
- **Request in-person**: Hans Buitendijk
- **Description**: The definition of the Composition.participant, including its binding, implies a unique need for documenting participants for FHIR Clinical Documents, not just FHIR Documents in general. It seems that this is overly restrictive. While in the FHIR Clinical Document context the binding would be appropriate, it seems to restrictive here.

Suggest that the binding is examplar, while the definition should replace "the clinical document" with "the document".

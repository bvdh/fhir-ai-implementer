- **Description**: The Request pattern reason should support a broader range of resource references representing why a request was made. We encountered this with ServiceRequest.reason, where we added FamilyMemberHistory, MedicationAdministration, MedicationRequest, and AllergyIntolerance (https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-58530). The same need likely applies to the broader Request pattern.

Examples:

FamilyMemberHistory: investigation prompted by family history
MedicationAdministration: testing to check effects or side effects after administration
MedicationRequest: baseline or monitoring tests associated with a medication order
AllergyIntolerance: investigation or alternative treatment prompted by an allergy

Proposal:
Review and expand the allowed reference types for Request reason. At minimum, consider: Procedure and DetectedIssue, but also consider resources such as FamilyMemberHistory and MedicationAdministration.

Also consider whether other resources such as DiagnosticReport, DocumentReference, AdverseEvent, or other request/event resources should be permitted.

The range of possible reasons for requests is broad, so the common Request pattern should not unnecessarily restrict valid clinical triggers or justifications.

- **Description**: There are examples where the period over which the medication has to be taken is defined as a startdate with a duration such as "take x starting on Jan. 1st 2026 for three months". This could be expressed by combining MedicationRequest.effectiveTimingDuration and MedicationRequest.effectiveTimingPeriod.start.

Suggest changing cardinality of MedicationRequest.effectiveTiming[x] to 0..*.

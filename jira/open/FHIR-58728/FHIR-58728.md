- **Description**: We use heavily Bundle.signature in R4 implementations, our transaction Bundles are signed and the signature is given in Bundle.signature. Luckily the ballot 6 didn't get enough affirmatives, because if we had noticed this change, our vote would have been negative because of this.

We have understood that the TU (trial use) was in spec because of the datatype Signature, its status was TU, not because Bundle.signature element was TU. Nowhere in the Bundle specification in versions R4, R4B, R5, R6 ballot 1, R6 ballot 2, R6 ballot 3 was earlier mentioned nothing textual or more specific about the trial use of signature element and what it excatly means! And in fact, in R6 ballot 2 and R6 ballot 3, the TU was removed and the Bundle.signature still existed.

Then in R6 ballot 4 was added text "This element is deprecated, and Provenance based Signatures should be used instead" and in ballot 5 Bundle.signature was removed. Where we can find discussions about this decision to remove the Bundle.signature, there are in ballot 6 links to the Jira tickets but there is nothing specific about this change.

So, this change is unacceptable to us.

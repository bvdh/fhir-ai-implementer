MedicationKnowledge

The MedicationKnowledge resource, which was part of FHIR R5, is not part of this release. Its content is now covered by other resources, depending on the purpose:

Purpose
Use

Identifying and describing a kind of medication, as a shared definition that other resources and systems can refer to
Medication. A Medication can optionally be a canonical resource (with url, version, name, status, publisher, jurisdiction and the other metadata elements), so that a Medication representing a kind of medication can be published, versioned and referenced. A Medication representing a physical instance can reference the Medication that represents its kind.

Information that is specific to a formulary, such as cost, coverage, dispensing limits and substitution rules
FormularyItem (an Additional Resource).

Detailed and regulatory product information, such as packaging, ingredients, clinical use, regulatory status and monitoring, and marketing authorization
MedicinalProductDefinition, PackagedProductDefinition, AdministrableProductDefinition, ManufacturedItemDefinition, Ingredient, ClinicalUseDefinition and RegulatedAuthorization. A Medication can reference these resources in Medication.definition.

Mapping from MedicationKnowledge

The following table shows where the content of each MedicationKnowledge (R5) element can be represented in this release.

MedicationKnowledge (R5)
Equivalent in this release

identifier
Medication.identifier

code
Medication.code

status
Medication.status

author
Medication.publisher and Medication.contact (Medication as a canonical resource)

jurisdiction
Medication.jurisdiction (Medication as a canonical resource)

name
Medication.name and Medication.title (Medication as a canonical resource)

relatedMedicationKnowledge
To be decided. A possible use is to document relationships between medications at different levels of granularity.

associatedMedication
Not needed: the information is held on the Medication itself.

productType
medication-type extension

monograph
To be decided.

preparationInstruction
medication-preparationInstruction extension

cost
FormularyItem.cost

monitoringProgram
MedicinalProductDefinition.additionalMonitoringIndicator; at formulary level, FormularyItem.monitoringProgram if needed

indicationGuideline
medication-indicationGuideline extension; indications as such in ClinicalUseDefinition, through Medication.definition

medicineClassification
Medication.classification

packaging
PackagedProductDefinition, through Medication.definition; packaging cost in FormularyItem.cost

clinicalUseIssue
ClinicalUseDefinition, through Medication.definition

storageGuideline
InventoryItem

regulatory.regulatoryAuthority
RegulatedAuthorization

regulatory.substitution
FormularyItem.substitution

regulatory.schedule
MedicinalProductDefinition (legalStatusOfSupply)

regulatory.maxDispense
FormularyItem.maxDispense (if needed)

definitional.definition
Medication.definition

definitional.doseForm
Medication.doseForm

definitional.intendedRoute
MedicinalProductDefinition.route or AdministrableProductDefinition.routeOfAdministration

Medication.ingredient (ingredient.type → Medication.ingredient.role; strength → Medication.ingredient.strength)

definitional.drugCharacteristic
medication-characteristic extension

(The modification is a section in the Medications module page instead of a separate page.)

Notes:

The mapping follows the Pharmacy WG meeting of 21 May 2026 at the Rotterdam WGM (mapping of MedicationKnowledge elements to other resources; minutes). relatedMedicationKnowledge and monograph were left for further discussion at that meeting.
Depends on: FHIR-57813 (Medication optionally a canonical resource; resolved) for author, jurisdiction and name; FHIR-59521 (adds Medication.classification); and FHIR-59522 (Extensions Pack: adds medication-preparationInstruction and medication-indicationGuideline). medication-type and medication-characteristic already exist.
As a section rather than a page, links to the R5 medicationknowledge.html will not land on it unless a redirect is set up at publication.
- **Description**: To facilitate implementers of the MedicationKnowledge, we need to provide a page that can be pointed to by FHIR R6 that indicates:

MedicationKnowledge has been depecrated in favour of a combination of FormularyItem, Medication, and MedicinalProductDefinition
Provides a mapping of the MedicationKnowledge elements to the other resources

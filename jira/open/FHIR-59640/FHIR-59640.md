- **Resolution Description**: We will correct the examples as proposed in the description:

medicationrequestexample4: correct the medication display and code (the immune globulin product, not Nystatin and not a procedure code), the route (SNOMED CT 47625008 "Intravenous route"), the UCUM units and the dates; structure the infusion as steps with conditions (90 minutes; 60 minutes if tolerated; 30 minutes if tolerated – see FHIR-59569) and give the example a matching title.
medicationrequest0349 / 0350: 0349 = do not prescribe amoxicillin, reason → the penicillin AllergyIntolerance; 0350 = doxycycline, reason = the indication as a condition, supportingInformation → medicationrequest0349, and a note ("Doxycycline as an alternative to amoxicillin because of a penicillin allergy"); remove the free-text reason "Alternative therapy due to penicillin allergy"; update both titles.
- **Description**: Two MedicationRequest examples do not show what their titles say, and contain errors.

1. medicationrequestexample4 (medrx004) – "Request with multiple dosages and timings"

The content does not match the title:

There is one step with one component and a single timing.event (2023-01-18) – no multiple dosages or timings.
The varying part is only in the text: "First treatment infuse over 90 minutes, if no reaction then second treatment infuse over 60 minutes, if no reaction then on third and following treatments give over 30 minutes." None of it is structured.
The two doseAndRate entries are the same dose expressed twice (ordered 5 mg/kg and calculated 340 mg) – a valid pattern, but not multiple dosages.

Other errors:

medication.reference.display is "Nystatin 100,000 u/ml oral suspension", but the contained Medication is GAMMAGARD Liquid (immune globulin).
The contained Medication.code is a SNOMED CT procedure (116805008 "Immune serum globulin administration by intravascular infusion"), not a medication product.
The route uses a proprietary code system (http://flatiron.com/CodeSystem/Route).
The doseQuantity values (mg/kg, mg) have no UCUM system/code.
timing.event (2023-01-18) is years after authoredOn (2015-01-15).

2. medicationrequest0349 / medicationrequest0350 – alternative therapy because of an allergy (from FHIR-51148)

medicationrequest0350 (doxycycline) has three reason entries – an AllergyIntolerance (penicillin allergy), the text "Alternative therapy due to penicillin allergy", and SNOMED CT 724053002 "Antibiotic therapy for prevention of recurrent infection (procedure)". This does not show "alternative to what":

The three reasons are a flat list; nothing links the "alternative therapy" reason to the allergy (only XML comments do).
The first-line medication (amoxicillin) is not represented, only mentioned in the allergy's display ("e.g., amoxicillin").
"Alternative therapy …" is not a reason or indication for ordering doxycycline; it is a remark about the choice of product.
The indication is a procedure code, where a condition is expected.
The companion medicationrequest0349 (doNotPerform, reason = the penicillin allergy) is for piperacillin/tazobactam, not amoxicillin, so the pair does not tell one story.

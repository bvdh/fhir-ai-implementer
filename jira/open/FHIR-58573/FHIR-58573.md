- **Resolution Description**: Change "comment" on https://build.fhir.org/group-definitions.html#Group.type to avoid recapitulating the entire code system. Instead say "Group members SHALL be of the appropriate resource type (e.g., Patient for person or animal), or a Group of the resources of the appropriate type."

This will help us avoid getting out of sync as the code system evolves in the future.

- **Description**: Group.type has this comment: "Group members SHALL be of the appropriate resource type (Patient for person or animal; or Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Device, CareTeam, HealthcareService, Location, Organization, RelatedPerson, Specimen, Medication, Substance, BiologicallyDerivedProduct, or NutritionProduct for the other types.), or a Group of the resources of the appropriate type."

But it's missing two Resource Type references that have a corresponding type code:

MedicinalProductDefinition for medicinalproductdefinition
SubstanceDefinition for substancedefinition

I suggest those two be added to the Comment.

### 2. David Barwin - 2026-09-23

It seems valid to continue searching for observations with category=vital-signs as we do today, recognizing that observations representing the defined vital signs need to use the specified LOINC codes to conform to those profiles.

Those profiles are not necessarily an exhaustive list of vital signs, so a site could have additional observations categorized as vital-signs that don’t conform to one of the defined profiles, and those could still be returned by the search.

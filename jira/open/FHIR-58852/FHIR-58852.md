- **Description**: The ValueSet signature-type expands two CodeSystems that both carry FHIR resource status active, but the external standards they encode do not appear to still be current:

ASTM E1762-95(2013), the base for the ASTM CodeSystem (urn:iso-astm:E1762-95:2013), was formally withdrawn by ASTM in 2017. This is shown as "Historical" on ANSI's webstore listing and as "Withdrawn 2017" on at least one ASTM reseller catalog. No replacement standard is listed.
ETSI TS 101 733 (CAdES) and TS 101 903 (XAdES), the bases for the ETSI CodeSystem (uri.etsi.org/01903/v1.2.2), are the older ETSI Technical Specification generation for these signature formats. ETSI has since published EN 319 122-1 (CAdES) and EN 319 132-1/-2 (XAdES) as the current European Norm versions in this space (from ~2015-2016 onward). However, third-party catalogs (GlobalSpec) still list the TS documents as "active," and EN 319 132-1 itself states that it builds on and extends TS 101 903's XML namespace rather than replacing it. So this is not a confirmed deprecation, just an open question since I can't confirm myself.

Ask:

Where withdrawal is confirmed (as with ASTM E1762-95), please update the CodeSystem status/description to reflect it - or evaluate deprecating the affected codes at the next ballot cycle - and note any current replacement for implementers. For the ETSI side, since our own check was inconclusive, we'd defer to FHIR-I/ETSI liaison judgment on whether equivalent treatment is warranted.

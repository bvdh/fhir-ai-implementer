- **Description**: The following list of resources are defined by Biomedical Research and Regulation and Pharmacy and are relevant to Devices. Yet the Device pages do not clearly indicate the way these resources are to be used with devices nor are all resource linked to DeviceDefinition.

Resource
Source workgroup
Description

ClinicalUseDefinition
Biomedical Research and Regulation
Records indications, contraindications, interactions, undesirable effects, and warnings for medications, substances, devices, procedures, biologically derived products, and nutrition products.

PackagedProductDefinition
Biomedical Research and Regulation
Describes medically related items in a package, including packaged medications, devices, food, biologicals, and other products.

RegulatedAuthorization
Biomedical Research and Regulation
Covers authorization for regulated products, explicitly including drugs, devices, biologically derived products, radiation-emitting products, nutrition products, and software.

ResearchStudy
Biomedical Research and Regulation
Supports studies involving medications, devices, therapies, and other interventional or investigative techniques.

ResearchSubject
Biomedical Research and Regulation
Allows the research subject to be a human, animal, device, drug product, biological product, substance, tissue, or other sample.

please indicate in the DeviceDefinition page the relationship with these resources.

Add description on the Device, DeviceDefinition or Device module page on how these resources can be used with Devices.

Also check for overlap for things defined in these resources with elements in devices and align where possible and applicable.

- **Description**: The provenance codes (used in .meta.security) have a distinction between reported by ({}SECINTPRVRBOBV{_} ) and asserted ( {}SECINTPRVABOBV{_} ). Yet this distinction is not defined. The distinction should be made more clear in the vocabulary but may also need to be made more clear elsewhere. I think that distinction is as follows:

In provenance, "reported by" and "asserted" refer to different aspects of how information is recorded and validated:

reported by: This refers to data that has been provided or supplied by an entity, but it does not necessarily imply verification or validation. A contribution can come from various sources, including automated systems, individuals, or organizations.

Asserted: This indicates that an entity has explicitly stated or confirmed the authenticity or accuracy of the information. An assertion carries a stronger implication of trust and verification compared to a mere contribution.

Essentially, a reported by is about supplying information, while an assertion is about validating or endorsing it.

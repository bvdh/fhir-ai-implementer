- **Description**: The text still says status generally "moves down through this list" from registered/preliminary → final → amended. The R6 code system is no longer a simple progression: partial is a parent of preliminary; amended has corrected and appended specializations; and cancelled, deprecated, entered-in-error, and unknown have distinct meanings.

This sentence was much more reasonable in R4, where there were only preliminary/final/amended/entered-in-error.

Proposal:

Rewrite as lifecycle guidance rather than "moves down the list." Explicitly explain that some codes are specializations rather than sequential states.

MedicationDispense.authorizingPrescription 0..* reference(MedicationRequest)

This raises a question of what use case(s) or clinical scenario(s) would support an instance of MedicationDispense referencing multiple authorizingPrescrption?

Here is a clinical example that can be added to provide the context:
Two distinct MedicationRequest orders for the meds to be take concurrently

rosuvastatin (a HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor statin) e.g.20 mg, or 40 mg
ezetimibe (cholesterol absorption inhibitor) e.g. 10 mg
Pharmacy can dispense a brand-named combination pack, e.g.:
Roszet (internationally) and Rosuzet, Ezalo, or
Pharmacor Ezetimibe Rosuvastatin Composite Pack in Australia

Suggest: added the above as json or xml examples to MedicationDispense

- **Resolution Description**: Updates to DeviceDefinition:

DeviceDefinition.material.substance

Element Id
DeviceDefinition.material.substance

Definition
A substance that the device contains, may contain, or is made of or manufactured from - for example latex - to be used to determine patient compatibility. This is not intended to represent the composition of the device, only the clinically relevant materials.

Short Display
A relevant substance that the device contains, may contain, or is made of

Comment
For substances placed within a device see .additive

DeviceDefinition.additive

Element Id
DeviceDefinition.additive

Definition
Material Substance added to a container device (typically used in specimen collection or initial processing). The material may be added by the device manufacturer or by a different party subsequent to manufacturing.

Short Display
Material Substance added to a container device

Updates to Device:

Device.additive

Element Id
Device.additive

Definition
Material Substance added to a container device (typically used in specimen collection or initial processing). The material may be added by the device manufacturer or by a different party subsequent to manufacturing.

Short Display
Material Substance added to a container device

- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Applied for Version**: R6
- **Description**: For DeviceDefinition, we should more clearly identify that additive is something that the device contains, while material is the composition of the container. This likely means improvements to the short and definition of both elements, and perhaps a more specific example in the comment (or more details in the example), to highlight that the additive is in the container.

Confirm if Device needs similar changes.

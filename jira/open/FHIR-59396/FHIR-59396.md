# FHIR-59396: Why can a patient or their carer not be a Group.characteristic.determiner?

- **Description**: The. definition Group.characteristic.determiner of is:

> The entity who is expected to evaluate the characteristic value for candidate group members.

Why can this not be a patient or their carer? Obviously in an institutional context, it's not going to be a patient or their carer, but there's nothing about Group that restricts it to institutional contexts.

Seems like we should add Patient and RelatedPerson here.

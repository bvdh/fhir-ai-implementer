- **Description**: `DocumentReference` does not provide an element for copyright or licensing information. Such metadata is important when documents are published, exchanged or reused, particularly in research and knowledge-sharing environments.

The `artifact-copyright` extension provides this information for several non-canonical resources, but `DocumentReference` is not currently included in its permitted contexts.

Please consider adding copyright and licensing metadata to the core `DocumentReference` structure or making an appropriate standard extension available.

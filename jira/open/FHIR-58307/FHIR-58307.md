- **Description**: Many vaccines are combination vaccines like MMR, DTP. They are aiming at different target diseases like measles, mumps, rubella. Even hepatitis B are targeting different strains of hepatitis B. The key question is here whether the evaluation is a kind of time event of the observation evaluation, or whether it is an evaluatio of the target diseases. If it is the event of the evaluation then it would be more logical that the target disease should be a 1..,many relationship and not a 1..1.
If it remains a 1..1 relationship then this evaluation could be a time consuming labor with a lot of repetition for each combination vaccine.

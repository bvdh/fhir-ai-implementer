- **Specification**: FHIR Core (FHIR)
- **Raised in Version**: R6
- **Work Group**: Pharmacy
- **Outstanding Negatives**: Normative
- **Related URL**: https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot4/dosage-examples.html#
- **Related Page(s)**: Dosage
- **Related Section(s)**: 14.7.1.3 example 2 Variant dosages at different times of the day, with cumulative dose limits
- **Resolution Description**: In Example 2, the as-needed component "5 mg PRN after 17:00 (max 2×/week)" has a dose limit with scope = administration, which means 2 tablets per administration. We will change it to scope = period with period = 1 week, in all representations. (The modification: the example does not have two dosage-level safety elements – one is on the as-needed component and one applies to the whole dosage – so no safety element is removed.)
- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: Example 2 (14.7.1.3) says "Methylphenidate 10 mg tablets — 10 mg at 07:30 and 12:00, 5 mg at 16:00 Mon-Fri; 10 mg at 08:00 Sat-Sun and holidays; 5 mg PRN after 17:00 (max 2×/week); do not exceed 150 mg per week.".

The last component in the step is meant to express " 5 mg PRN after 17:00 (max 2x/week)". However, component.safety.doseLimit in the example does not contain a period. The component.safety.doseLimit.scope is set to "administration", which, according to 14.7.0.4 DosageSafety, defines a dose limit that applies for a single administration. This is interpreted as max 2 tablets for a single administration and not as max 2 tablets per week.

The suggestion is to set the scope to period and add period to safety.doseLimit in this example. See below:

<component>
<asNeeded value="true"/>
<timing>
<repeat>
<when value="EVE"/>
</repeat>
</timing>
<doseAndRate>
<doseQuantity>
<value value="5"/>
<unit value="mg"/>
<system value="http://unitsofmeasure.org"/>
<code value="mg"/>
</doseQuantity>
</doseAndRate>
<safety>
<doseLimit>
<valueQuantity>
<value value="2"/>
<unit value="tablet"/>
<system value="http://unitsofmeasure.org"/>
<code value="tbl"/>
</valueQuantity>
<scope value="period"/>
<period>
<value value="1"/>
<unit value="week"/>
<system value="http://unitsofmeasure.org"/>
<code value="wk"/>
</period>
</doseLimit>
</safety>
</component>

Example 2 has two dosageInstruction.safety elements, this is double and has to be only one.

- **Description**: The meaning of CarePlan.custodian is unclear and potentially misleading.

In FHIR, custodian is commonly understood as the entity responsible for the custody and maintenance of information (e.g., Composition.custodian). The definition of CarePlan.custodian ("Who is the designated responsible party") instead appears to describe the individual, organization, or team professionally responsible for the care plan and its execution.

Please clarify the intended semantics and the distinction from author, contributor, and care team members. If the intent is accountability for the plan rather than custody of the information, a different element name should be considered to avoid implementation ambiguity.

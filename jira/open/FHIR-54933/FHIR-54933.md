- **Description**: Radiology reports (and presumably other diagnostic processes) often make assertions of the absence of a condition that include a degree of certainty/confidence. Some imaging is specifically done with the primary goal of ruling out some condition or other.

There needs to be a way to record those degrees of certainty. Consider allowing qualitative (low confidence, moderate, high, etc) and quantitative (80% likely) values. E.g. Diagnostic Certainty Scale - RAD-ASSIST

AI Models and other Clinical applications may also output certainty metrics.

This JIRA relates to FHIR-54931 Support degree of confidence/certainty that a Condition is present - Jira. Presence of a condition is encoded as a Condition while absence of a condition is encoded as an Observation (unless it's refuted, resolved, inactive, in remission, or entered-in-error).

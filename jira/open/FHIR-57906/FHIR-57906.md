- **Description**: Description:
Ballot 5 adds constraint spm-1: "Specimen.collection.collector SHALL only be present if Specimen.collection.procedure is not present." This appears to be new in R6 — it isn't present in the R5 StructureDefinition, and it isn't listed in the resource's R5-to-R6 change notes either, so it's easy to miss.

The constraint prevents sending a collector alongside a collection procedure, but that's a valid and common combination. Systems routinely record both who physically collected the specimen and a reference to the associated collection procedure/order, and the two aren't necessarily reconciled — this is especially true for interfaced and historical data. As written, spm-1 would force implementers to drop the collector (losing who drew the sample) whenever a collection procedure is also referenced. That's real data we can no longer represent.

If the intent is "when a Procedure is present, use its performer instead," that's reasonable as guidance but too strong as a hard invariant.

Proposed resolution:
Remove spm-1, or downgrade it from an error to a warning/best-practice note so both elements may be present. If it's retained in any form, please also add it to the R5-to-R6 change list, since a new SHALL constraint is exactly the kind of change implementers need to see.

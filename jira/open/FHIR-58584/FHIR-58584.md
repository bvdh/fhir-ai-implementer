The patient Problem List seems important enough that it should be explicit and should be curated, managed and used intentionally rather be a side-effect of a category tag.

Could consider profiling the List resource, and reference Conditions, but first should discuss what metadata would be useful. Who is responsible for the list? When was it last reviewed? When was it last changed. Who are the intended users for this list?

The last one presumes that different physicians might want to work off of different problem lists. Different sites might not want to share/sync a single list. They might want to prioritize differently. The orthopedic clinic doesn’t want to scroll down through a dozen items that are completely irrelevant to physiotherapy. Psychiatric problem list might be kept separate from the oncology list. The family doctor might want to curate their problem list for the patient rather than having it automatically contain everything from all the other specialists.

- **Description**: `Questionnaire.name` represents a computer-friendly name, while `Questionnaire.title` represents a single human-friendly title. However, standardized questionnaires are frequently known by acronyms, short titles and other alternative names that cannot be distinguished using the current elements.

Consider adding a repeatable complex element based on `ResearchStudy.label`, with elements such as `type`, `value` and `language`. This would allow acronyms, alternative titles and translated titles to be represented.

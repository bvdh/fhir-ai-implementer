- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Compatible, substantive
- **Description**: Description:

The human description of ist-4 reads:
"If numberOfInstances and series.instance are both present, the numberOfInstances value SHALL match the total number of series.instance elements"
The FHIRPath does not implement that condition:

numberOfInstances.exists() and series.exists() implies numberOfInstances = series.instance.count()

Requested change: Align the guard with the human description:

numberOfInstances.exists() and series.instance.exists() implies numberOfInstances = series.instance.count()

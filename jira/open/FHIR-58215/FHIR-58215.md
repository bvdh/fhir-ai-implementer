- **Description**: The Claim resource includes a Claim.related element used to relate the claim to prior submissions or related services.

The ClaimResponse resource should include a ClaimResponse.related element to allow insurers the flexibility to tie this payment/response with a previous response. For example, if an insurer re-adjudicates specific services/items from a claim (e.g. paying after initially denying), this would allow both parties to tie prior payments together more easily. It would also allow handling for partial adjudications.

In https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-53935, it was stated that if a Claim is reversed, all prior payments are reversed. I believe this was a misinterpretation of my comment. Insurers will reverse payments on their own, without new claim submission. I would not expect the reversal or re-issuing of a ClaimResponse to fully nullify a Claim.

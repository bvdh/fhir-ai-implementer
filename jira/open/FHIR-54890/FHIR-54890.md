- **Description**: Consider observations that record:

the measured head circumference of a Fetus B of mother Patient A.
the measured diameter of a tumor in the liver of Patient A
the measured diameter of a stent in the descending coronary artery of Patient A.
the measured diameter of the descending coronary artery of Patient A where the stent is placed.

It seems logical that

.subject= Patient A, .focus= Fetus B, .code = Circumference, .bodyStructure = Head
.subject= Patient A, .code= Diameter, .bodyStructure.morphology= tumor/.include=liver
.subject= Patient A, .focus= Device(Stent), .code=Diameter,
.subject= Patient A, .code= Diameter, .bodyStructure= DCA

Might need to clarify the .bodyStructure Definition:

"Indicates the body structure on the subject's body of the subject (or the focus if present) where the observation was made (i.e. the target site)."

since the bodyStructure is the head of the fetus not the head of the mother.

And the .focus Comment bullets seem misleading:

A parent is trained to change their child's tracheostomy tube. The child is the subject, and the parent is the focus.

A fetal heart rate recorded in a mother's record. The mother is the subject, and the fetus is the focus.

Features of a body structure, such as a tumor. The patient is the subject, and the specific body structure is the focus.

Metrics captured from a medical device. The patient-attached device is the subject, the specific device metric is the focus, and the observing device itself is referenced in .device.

The first two sound right. The third bullet does not sound right. BodyStructure handles and includes morphological abnormalities like tumors. The fourth bullet is confusing. There seem to be two devices (described as a medical device, a patient-attached device, a specific device and an observing device) and a patient, but the patient does not seem to be captured and it's not clear whats going on between the devices. An example would help.

## Comments

### 1. KevinO'Donnell - 2026-09-25

A separate JIRA can be created to consider capturing relevant device settings during an observation on a patient. These will likely go in .context, which is used to capture potentially relevant state of the patient (fasting, standing) or the device (sampling mode, sensitivity) when an observation takes place.

### 2. KevinO'Donnell - 2026-09-25

Proposed modification #1: Revise Observation.bodyStructure Definition as follows:

"Indicates the body structure on the subject's body of the subject (or the focus if present) where the observation was made (i.e. the target site)."

Proposed modification #2: Revise Observation.focus Comment as follows:

A parent is trained to change their child's tracheostomy tube. The child is the subject, and the parent is the focus.
A fetal heart rate recorded in a mother's record. The mother is the subject, and the fetus is the focus.
Features of a body structure, such as a tumor. The patient is the subject, and the specific body structure is the focus.
A pressure reading from a pneumatic tourniquet recorded in the patient's record. The patient is the subject, and the tourniquet device is the focus. The Observation.code would indicate a pressure observation. A performance assessment of Metrics captured from a medical device. The patient-attached device is the subject, the specific device metric is the focus, and the observing device itself is referenced in .device.

Features, such as a tumor, that are structures in the body of the subject do not use .focus, but rather are encoded using .morphology inside a BodyStructure resource referenced from Observation.bodyStructure.

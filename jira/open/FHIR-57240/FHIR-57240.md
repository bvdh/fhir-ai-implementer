- **Description**: It is not very clear if EpisodeOfCare is meant to cross over multiple organizations.

To be clear, I am not seeking to make this work across organizations, because that seems to make it overlap too much with Condition resource. I am looking for clarity.

The resource does seem to be oriented to single organization in its definition:

"An association between a patient and an organization / healthcare provider(s) during which time encounters may occur. The managing organization assumes a level of responsibility for the patient during this time."

"An" and "the" both imply singular.

Also there is this text "Many organizations can be involved in an EpisodeOfCare; however each organization will have its own EpisodeOfCare resource instance that tracks its responsibility with the patient(s)."

But that is unclear and self contradictory. Is the first "EpisodeOfCare" in the text meant to not be the resource but the general concept of "episode of care"? Or does it mean there should be one EpisodeOfCare resource for each organization plus another for all of them together? Or is the nuance that "involvement" is not "responsibility"?

The next sentence isn't grammatical and seems to end too soon:
"When an Organization completes their involvement with the patient(s) and transfers care to another Organization. " (stops).

Is it intended to say something like " ... this would be generate a new EpisodeOfCare resource instance". That would be consistent with one EpisodeOfCare per organization.

Also the relationship between EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization and EpisodeOfCare.careTeam is not very clear. EpisodeOfCare.careTeam seems to only mention practitioners, not organizations: "The list of practitioners that may be facilitating this episode of care for specific purposes.". So, team is practitioners.

Yet EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization says:
"If multiple organizations are involved in care delivery, the care teams from those organizations would be represented in EpisodeOfCare.team, while the primary organization managing the care would be listed in EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization"

That seems to imply multiple organizations, not just practitioners.

I understand that a list of practitioners could be from different organizations, but that is not quite the same as a list of organizations themselves (which implies some ownership by that organization).

Also, why is managingOrganization 0..1?

From the definition, it seems like there has to be an organization. Why then is that not 1..1?

Is it because there may not be one at all (no organization is giving care)?
Or because there is always is one (and only one) but you don't have to mention it?

That doesn't seem good practice for an interop spec, where the resource may cross between organizations one day. I can see that it may be overkill to mention the organization in every instance if this resource is always embedded in a system owned by one organization.

But equally, if the organization is always obvious and doesn't need to be mentioned (or is mentioned as a generic "the health system") then the use of this resource becomes just "there is something we care about for this patient".

That functionally too similar to Condition and these must be clearly differentiated.

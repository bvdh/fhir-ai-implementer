- **Description**: Definition
The subject of record that the specimen is associated with. This subject may be a patient, group, location, device, or another entity. If the specimen is collected from an entity different from the subject of record (Specimen.subject), that entity should be recorded using Specimen.focus. For example, a specimen may be collected from an animal for the purpose of providing care to a person rather than the animal itself.

Short Display
Who or what this specimen is from (subject of record)

Short Display and Definition do not match. I would assume a better definition would be to change the "Who or what this specimen is from" to something similar along the lines of "Who or what this specimen was obtain for"

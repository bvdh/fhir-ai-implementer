# FHIR-37783: Discourage the use of CommunicationReques

- **Resolution Vote**: Mark Scrimshire / Gail Kocher: 6 - 0 - 1
- **Vote Date**: 2026-09-21
- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Applied for Version**: R6
- **Description**: Background and Rationale:

Da Vinci HREX IG (http://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-hrex/exchanging-request.html#request), documents the downsides associated with Requesting exchange using CommunicationRequest, and DaVinci CDex uses a Task based approach instead.

See also FHIR-37228
Proposal:

We propose changing the Financial Management Group page to expand the list of options to include Task as outlined in the HRex guide and to discourage the use of CommunicationRequest.

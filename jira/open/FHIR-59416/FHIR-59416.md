- **Description**: Summary: Add participant type codes for carers, support persons and informants so non-professional participants in an encounter can be typed.

Artifacts: THO CodeSystem participant-type (http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/participant-type); core ValueSet encounter-participant-type (also used by Appointment.participant.type)

Description: Encounter.participant.actor allows RelatedPerson, but encounter-participant-type contains only v3 _ParticipationAncillary roles (admitter, attender, callback, consultant, discharger, escort, referrer) plus translator and emergency. There is no code for the most common non-professional participants in home, community and social care encounters — a family or other carer, a support person or advocate, or a person providing information about the subject. Implementers either leave type empty or invent local codes.

Proposed resolution:

Add to THO participant-type:

caregiver — "A person, usually not acting as a healthcare professional, who provides care or support to the subject (e.g. family member, friend or paid carer) and participates in the encounter in that capacity."
support-person — "A person who accompanies the subject to provide support, advocacy or companionship, without providing care in the encounter."
informant — "A person who provides information about the subject during the encounter." (Alternatively, include v3-ParticipationType INF in the value set instead of a new code — WG to decide.)

Include the new codes in ValueSet encounter-participant-type.

Impact: Compatible (additive codes; binding is preferred as of FHIR-55649).

- **Resolution Description**: We will add HealthcareService and Location as allowed dispenseRequest.dispenser targets.
- **Description**: MedicationRequest.dispenseRequest.dispenser is currently restricted to Organization.

1. A dispensing pharmacy may instead be represented as a HealthcareService or a specific Location within a hospital, rather than as a separate Organization.

2. A hospital may have multiple internal pharmacy services, such as central, ambulatory, ICU, or emergency pharmacies, while all of them remain part of the same Organization.

Therefore, Organization alone may not be sufficient to identify the actual dispensing service or location.

Proposal:
Consider allowing HealthcareService and/or Location as valid references for dispenseRequest.dispenser.

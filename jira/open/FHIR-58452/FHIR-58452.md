- **Description**: In the Constraints table for SearchParameter (and other resource types), the invariant identifiers in the UniqueKey column are preceded by different icons. For example, some entries have a green tick, while spd-4 and spd-5 have a grey minus.

The meaning of these icons is not explained on the page or in the general documentation for constraints. The icons also use the non-descriptive alternative text and do not provide an explanatory tooltip.

Please document the meaning (internal test coverage?) of the icons that may appear in this table.

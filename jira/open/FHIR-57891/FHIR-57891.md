- **Description**: Change:

IG authors looking to publish implementation guides within HL7 International, whether for international or U.S. specific use should follow these guidelines but are also bound to a set of additional policies set by various HL7 governance bodies. For these policies, authors should consult the HL7 International Terminology Playbook on HL7 Confluence which summarizes FAQs relating these HL7 International-specific policies.

To:

This guidance applies to all IG authors. However, anyone publishing implementation guides through HL7 International infrastructure or tooling — whether for international or U.S.-specific use — must also comply with policies set by HL7 governance bodies, including the Terminology Services Management Group (TSMG). For HL7-specific policies, authors should consult: - The HL7 International Terminology Playbook (https://confluence.hl7.org/display/TSMG/Terminology+Play+Book) - Terminology Expectations for IG Developers (https://confluence.hl7.org/spaces/TSMG/pages/161063724/Terminology+Expectations+for+IG+Developers)

Because:

Two issues: (1) The current text scopes the additional policy requirements narrowly to authors "looking to publish within HL7 International," which understates the reach — the guidance and TSMG policies apply broadly to anyone publishing through HL7 infrastructure or tooling. (2) The Terminology Expectations for IG Developers page on TSMG Confluence is directly relevant to this document's audience and should be listed alongside the Terminology Playbook as a required reference.

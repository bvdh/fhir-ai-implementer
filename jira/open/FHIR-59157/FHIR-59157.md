- **Description**: Other request/order resources that have the asNeeded/asNeededFor element all appear to have a 0..* cardinality. DeviceRequest has 0..1. It is conceivable that a device may be ordered to support multiple conditions. Relax the cardinality for DeviceRequest.asNeededFor to 0..*.

- **Description**: Add a new, repeatable clinicalImpact element to DetectedIssue to represent the anticipated clinical impact of a detected issue.

Element: DetectedIssue.clinicalImpact
Cardinality: 0..*
Type: CodeableConcept
Short: Clinical impact of the detected issue

Definition:

A coded characterization of the anticipated clinical impact of the detected issue, such as an increase or decrease in therapeutic effect, an adverse reaction, additive adverse effects, a recommendation to avoid concurrent use, or a contraindication that applies to some patients.

The element is repeatable because a single detected issue may have more than one clinical impact.

Proposed Terminology

Create an example-bound value set, tentatively named Detected Issue Clinical Impact.

Suggested initial concepts are:

Code
Display
Definition

increased-effect
Increased effect
The clinical or pharmacologic effect of one or more implicated therapies is expected to be increased.

decreased-effect
Decreased effect
The clinical or pharmacologic effect of one or more implicated therapies is expected to be decreased.

mixed-effect
Mixed effect
Mixed or differing changes in clinical or pharmacologic effects are expected.

adverse-reaction
Adverse reaction
An adverse reaction is expected or its risk is increased.

additive-adverse-effects
Additive adverse effects
Adverse or undesirable effects of the implicated therapies are expected to be additive.

avoid-concurrent-use
Avoid concurrent use when possible
Concurrent use should generally be avoided because of the identified clinical risk.

contraindicated-in-some-patients
Contraindicated in some patients
Concurrent use is contraindicated for a subset of patients based on patient-specific clinical characteristics or circumstances.

An Example binding is suggested so that implementations can also communicate terminology-specific clinical impact concepts where appropriate.

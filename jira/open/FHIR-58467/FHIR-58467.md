- **Description**: Specimen.processing.performer is currently restricted to Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Patient and RelatedPerson.
Specimen processing may also be performed by a laboratory or other healthcare service, which may be represented by an Organization or HealthcareService.
Consider allowing Organization and HealthcareService as valid reference targets.

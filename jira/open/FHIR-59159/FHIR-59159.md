DeviceRequest.asNeeded

Definition
This status is to indicate whether the request is a PRN or may be given as needed. for a device to be used as needed (a.k.a. PRN or pro re nata).

Short Display
PRN status of request Whether the device is to be used as needed

- **Description**: The definition of DeviceRequest.asNeeded/asNeededFor refers to "PRN" without explaining what that is, and says "This status is to indicate whether the request is a PRN or may be given as needed.".

Since PRN means "as needed" that definition is redundant. Both terms should be included, but not in a way to suggest they are alternate justifications.

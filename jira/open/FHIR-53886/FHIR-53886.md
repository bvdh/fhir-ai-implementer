- **Description**: This artifact includes several elements with overlapping functionality, such as provision, policyBasis, policyText, sourceAttachment, sourceReference, and RegulatoryBasis. Further explanation may be necessary to clearly define the distinctions and relationships among these components, including whether any element is depend on another and if all can coexist simultaneously— and if so, clarification on their respective roles.

For instance:

6.2.1.1 specifies that the Consent resource provides two mechanisms for recording computable rules:

the provision structure
the policyBasis

This appears to suggest that they are separate schemes and should not be used together. If this interpretation is correct, it would be beneficial to establish constraints to reinforce this separation.

However, section 6.2.6 states: "Policies attached to PolicyBasis should be written using positive language. For an example, a patient wanting to opt-out will be recorded with an opt-in policy where the Consent.provision[0].type indicates 'deny'." This seems to imply that provision may depend on policyBasis.

Another point of clarification:

What distinguishes policyText from sourceAttachment or sourceReference?

When including a privacy notice as a basis for consent, which element should be utilized?

# FHIR-59386: Should Composition.custodian allow for Patient + RelatedPerson?

- **Updated**: 2026-09-23

- **Type**: Change Request
- **Reporter**: Grahame Grieve

- **Specification**: FHIR Core (FHIR)
- **Raised in Version**: R6
- **Work Group**: Structured Documents
- **Related Artifact(s)**: Composition
- **Description**: Composition.custodian is defined as:

> Identifies the organization or group who is responsible for ongoing maintenance of and access to the composition/document information.

this is defacto an assertion that there cannot be a PHR, or at least, if there is, a patient cannot make a composition in that PHR, and/or a patient cannot manage a composition in their own right.

I believe that this is very much not the case, and that the patient should be able to be their own custodian. Further, if a patient can be, so can their designated carer: RelatedPerson.

Please add both to custodian.

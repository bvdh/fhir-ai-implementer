## Comments

### 1. David Barwin - 2026-10-15

Scope for this ticket: Add Device to Observation.performer AND introduce text on Device Scope and Usage with something to the effect of:

The use of Device to represent non-human actors or participants, including artificial intelligence (AI) systems, is an evolving area of FHIR implementation. Implementers should consider applicable jurisdictional requirements and seek guidance from relevant local authorities when determining whether and how Device may be used in an actor or participant capacity, including the appropriate boundaries and relationships with other resources such as Practitioner and PractitionerRole.

### 5. KevinO'Donnell - 2026-09-23

.performer

The entity responsible for the observation
The entity responsible for the validity and quality of the observation. E.g. the observing human, or software??
A group of Practitioners should be represented with practitioner-oriented resources like CareTeam or Organization, not Group. Group may be used to represent a family or a household.

.device

Device used to obtain the observation.
The primary tool or equipment used to obtain the observation. E.g. a microscope, chemical analyzer, or weigh scale.
This may capture the specific device, the class of device, or nothing at all.
?? or device settings? Why isn’t this a separate element?
Devices involved in the transmission of the result, e.g., a gateway, may be documented using the Provenance resource, not here.

.supportingDevice

Device(s) that supported obtaining the observation.
Device(s) used in concert with the primary device to obtain an accurate observation. E.g. a reagent kit, a calibration device, or a set of ECG leads

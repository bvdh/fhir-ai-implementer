- **Resolution Description**: We will replace the text as follows (strikethrough - remove; bold - add):

This resource covers all type of orders for medications for a patient. This includes inpatient medication orders as well as community orders (whether filled by the prescriber or by a pharmacy). It also includes orders for over-the-counter medications (e.g., Aspirin), total parenteral nutrition and diet/vitamin supplements. It may can also be used to support the order of medication-related devices e.g., prefilled syringes such as patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) syringes, or syringes used to administer other types of medications. e.g., insulin, narcotics such as prefilled syringes (for example, pre-filled insulin syringes) . It can can also be used to order medication or substances NOT be taken.

This resource would not be used when ordering a device(s) is not intended to be used to order devices that may have a medication coating e.g. heparin coated stints, or similar types of devices stents. These *Those *types of devices would be ordered using the Device Request or the SupplyRequest resources.

It This resource is not intended for use in prescribing particular -diets (see NutritionOrder), or for ordering -non-medication-related items (eyeglasses, supplies, etc.) items that are not related to medications, such as eyeglasses or supplies. In addition, the MedicationRequest may be used to report orders/request from external systems that have been reported for informational purposes and are not authoritative and are not expected to be acted upon (e.g. dispensed or administered). MedicationRequest can also be used to report orders or requests from other systems for informational purposes; such records are not authoritative and are not expected to be acted upon (for example, by dispensing or administering the medication).

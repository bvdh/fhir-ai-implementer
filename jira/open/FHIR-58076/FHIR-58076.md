- **Description**: Considering that the Composition.participant definition states that it "identifies supporting entities, including parents, relatives, caregivers, insurance policyholders, guarantors, and others related in some way to the patient" it may be helpful to the difference between participants and subject/focus more clearly. That could be done in the Composition about multiple entities, or perhaps better in the comments of .participants with an example or two of the functions included in .participant.function of the kind of support they provide

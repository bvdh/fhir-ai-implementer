- **Resolution Description**: The word "except" is still found in the explanation of provisions. This word needs to be changed to better reflect the alternating provision structure.
The alternating permit vs deny is only explained in a "For Example". This needs to be stated as a SHALL at the top of the provisions section.
This alternating needs to be stated in the definition of .provision and included in the short

- **Description**: The Consent.provision.provision definitions states "Nested provisions to further constraint the parent provision" yet the description under the first diagram in https://build.fhir.org/consent.html#provisions states "For example, if the base decision for a consent is permit, the first level of provisions express exceptions to this decision, " and under second diagram in https://build.fhir.org/consent.html#provisions states "The subsequent child provisions state exceptions to or constraints on this base rule. ".

Since the child provisions can either state constraint or state exceptions, the Consent.provision.provision attribute should represent both uses, not just "constraints".

Suggest to define as "Nested provisions that state further constraint or exceptions to the parent provision."

### 1. Mohammad Jafari - 2026-09-14

It seems like there is an assumption here about a technical difference between "constraint" and "exception". These terms are used generically in the specs and no well-defined difference between them was intended.

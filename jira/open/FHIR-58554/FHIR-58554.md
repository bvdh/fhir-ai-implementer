- **Description**: The definition and short display of `ResearchStudy.progressStatus` currently include "status changes for the conduct of the study, or study registry and administrative actions." This creates overlap with the `ResearchStudy StudyRegistration` extension, which specifically represents registration activities.

Please establish a clear distinction between:

states relating to study conduct and recruitment, represented by `progressStatus`
registration events such as submission, verification, quality review, and posting

Preferably, the existing StudyRegistration extension should be promoted to a core `ResearchStudy` element, and the definition of `progressStatus` should be limited to the conduct of the study. The respective CodeSystems and ValueSets should also be reviewed for overlapping concepts.

`Provenance` may supplement this model by documenting actual registration events and their agents, but it does not replace the need to represent anticipated registration activities directly on the study.

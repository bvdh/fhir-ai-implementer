If emptyReason is present, codableConcept SHALL contain CodableConcept.coding or CodableConcept.text explaining the empty reason.

In addition, change the Definition to more of a definition as opposed to what sounds like a comment, from:

If the section is empty, why the list is empty. An empty section typically has some text explaining the empty reason.

To:

The reason the section contains no content. For an empty section, emptyReason SHALL include narrative text or coded information identifying the reason for the absence of content.

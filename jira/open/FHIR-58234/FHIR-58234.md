- **Resolution Description**: In the Introduction of the Device Module update as follows:

This module provides guidance for representing medical and personal health devices, their characteristics, operational status, patient associations, utilization, and measurements. It also supports clinical workflows including device traceability, inventory management, patient-device association, device recalls, remote monitoring, and quality investigations.

Within FHIR a Device (link) can represent a diverse range of things such as MRIs, beds, tongue depressors, bandages, stents and implants, blood pressure monitors, weight scales, infusion pumps, clinical decision support software, continuous glucose monitors, and bedside monitors. Because of the range of possible devices and the range of uses, systems may choose to implement a subset of device-related resources.

Also add a links to all named resources in the module.

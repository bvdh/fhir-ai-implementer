- **Description**: Multiple Observations need to be captured over time on an entity that is a morphological abnormality (e.g. a specific tumor has its diameter and volume measured at several points in time.). Ideally, a BodyStructure instance represents that tumor entity and has a unique identifier. Each of the Observations reference the same BodyStructure instance. (They might also reference different BodyStructure instances that share a common business identifier, but in either case, the BodyStructure is representing the tumor entity. It happens to be located mostly inside the left kidney).

There seem to be two views on the usage of .morphology in BodyStructure.

1) .morphology identifies a morphological abnormality (entity) whose location is provided in .includedStructure. (i.e. there is a tumor that is located at the left kidney)

2) .morphology identifies characteristics of tissue identified in .includedStructure. (i.e. there is a left kidney that has tumorous tissue)

The primary job of BodyStructure is to identify an entity. To attribute characteristics to an entity is the job of Observation.

Proposal: Provide guidance that 1) is intended. To achieve 2), use Observations to attributes characteristics to a referenced (anatomical) BodyStructure.

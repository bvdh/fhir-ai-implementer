- **Request in-person**: Elliot Silver
- **Description**: The issue of how to document additives in specimen containers came up in https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-49741.

During discussion it was realized that medicated stents, and antibiotic-infused catheters provide similar challenges.

Consider adding a backbone element additive 0..* CodeableReference(Substance Medication) to describe.

(Do we need to track just the substance, or the dose of it as well?)

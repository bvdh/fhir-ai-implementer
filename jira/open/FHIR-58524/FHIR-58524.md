- **Description**: "Relevant date" is too ambiguous for interoperability. It is unclear whether the date represents:

submission date,
case opening date,
assessment period,
decision date,
case closure date, or
another regulatory milestone.

The example uses a Period for the case and a dateTime for each application, suggesting intended semantics, but those semantics are not stated in the element definition.

Suggested change:

Expand the definition of case.date[x] to explain what the date or period represents. For example, clarify whether a Period represents the duration of the regulatory case/procedure and whether a dateTime on an application represents its submission or receipt date.

Possible wording:

"The date or period during which this regulatory case or application is active or processed."

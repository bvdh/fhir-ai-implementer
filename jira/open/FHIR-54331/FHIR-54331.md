- **Description**: The given Dosage Regime examples in https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot4/dosage-examples.html use both Timing.repeat.count and Timing.repeat.boundsDuration to express the intended time duration of a DosageInstruction.step.component.

In example 3 (14.7.1.4) the elements Timing.repeat.count and Timing.repeat.boundsDuration are used interchangeably to express "for 5 days", "for 10 days" and "for 3 months". In Timing] the element Timing.repeat.count is described as the number of times to repeat, which expresses "repeat 5 times" or "repeat 10 times" and not "for 5 days" or "for 10 days".

Suggestion is to only use Timing.repeat.boundsDuration to express the intended time duration and remove Timing.repeat.count from the examples and only use Timing.repeat.boundsDuration instead.

- **Description**: `ResearchStudy.progressStatus` represents a repeating status history, while `ResearchStudy.whyStopped` is a single root-level element. It is therefore not possible to determine directly which status transition the stop reason applies to. Consumers must infer this from the sequence and timing of the status entries.

Please consider moving or adding `whyStopped` as an element of `ResearchStudy.progressStatus`, so that the state, its timing, and the corresponding reason are represented together. This would also support temporary closures, resumptions, and multiple relevant status changes.

If `whyStopped` remains at the resource root, an invariant or warning should ensure that it is only present when `progressStatus.state` contains an applicable premature-stop status, such as `terminated` or `withdrawn`. Because the binding of `progressStatus.state` is preferred, the feasibility of an invariant based on specific codes should also be reviewed.

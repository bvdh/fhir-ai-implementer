# FHIR-58570: Clarify representation of bibliographic citations in Composition.relatesTo

- **Related Artifact(s)**: Composition
- **Description**: In R6, `Composition.relatesTo` supports relationship types such as `citation`, `cites` and `cited-by`, while `target[x]` permits `uri`, `Attachment`, `canonical`, `Reference(Any)` and `markdown`.

This appears to cover many use cases of the `RelatedArtifact` datatype, but it is not clear how bibliographic references should be represented consistently when more structured citation metadata is required.

Please provide guidance on representing bibliographic citations through `Composition.relatesTo`.

- **Description**: Description:
With Specimen.focus added in R6 to represent the entity a specimen was collected from when that differs from the subject of record, the short description of Specimen.subject — "Who or what this specimen is from (subject of record)" — now conflicts with it. Read on its own, "who or what this specimen is from" describes the collection source, which is exactly what focus is for ("The entity from which the specimen was collected…"). The full definition of subject was correctly updated to explain the subject-of-record vs. focus distinction, but the short description still leads with "from" and undercuts that distinction. Since the resource is proposed Normative, the concept should be unambiguous at the label level.

Proposed resolution:
Revise the subject short description to align with its own definition and stop implying collection source — e.g., "The subject of record this specimen is associated with." Avoid framing subject as the "focus"/"target of testing," since that would collide with the focus element name.

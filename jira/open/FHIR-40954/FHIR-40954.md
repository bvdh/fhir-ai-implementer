- **Related Section(s)**: 5.1.0.8, 5.1.0.9
- **Description**: In 5.1.0.8 Profiling descriptive elements, the table indicates that mappings can be removed. However, 5.1.0.9 Using StructureDefinitions says:
Note that structure definitions cannot 'remove' mappings and constraints that are defined in the base structure, but for purposes of clarity, they can refrain from repeating them.
This was raised on Zulip here, and lloyd indicated that mappings definitely can be removed and that this should be clarified in 5.1.0.9.

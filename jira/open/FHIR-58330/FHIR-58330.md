- **Description**: According to isSummary:
In resource and datatype definitions, if an element has a descendant within the same StructureDefinition marked as isSummary=true or if it satisfies both of the following conditions, it must be marked as isSummary=true: *
is at the root or has a parent that is 'mustSupport'

has a minimum cardinality of 1 or is a modifier element

Because both Evidence.variableDefinition and Evidence.statistic don't satisfy the "has a minimum cardinality of 1" or "is a modifier element" and they both have a descendant that have isSummary, then that needs to be fixed.

So in order to fix this, Evidence.variableDefinition and Evidence.statistic must also be marked as isSummary as well.

- **Description**: The base profile for vital signs contains a lot of mustSupport elements in combination with conditions. This is primarily introduced to separate between one-value and two-value specializations like body weight or blood pressure.

It would be much easier to solve this problem by inserting another level into this two-level hierarchy that separates between those two basic sets.

An idea about how that may look like can be found at Use of Profile Hierarchies - Vital Signs Cross-Country Cross-Language Cross-Paradigm (Demo) IG v0.6.4

That will eliminate most of the conditions (I haven't checked them all) and clearly indicate the mustSupport elements. For example, it will indicate observation.value for one-value artefacts, and observation.component.value for two-value artefacts.

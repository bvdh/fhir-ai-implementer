# FHIR-59390: DetectedIssue.mitigation.author should allow for Patient and RelatedPerson

- **Work Group**: Clinical Decision Support
- **Related Artifact(s)**: DetectedIssue
- **Description**: The scope of detected issue is:

> Indicates an actual or potential clinical issue with or between one or more active or proposed clinical actions for a patient; e.g. Drug-drug interaction, Ineffective treatment frequency, Procedure-condition conflict, gaps in care, etc.

The definition of DetectedIssue.mitigation.author is:

> Identifies the practitioner who determined the mitigation and takes responsibility for the mitigation step occurring.

and the target types are: Practitioner | PractitionerRole

This casts the use of DetectedIssue into an institutional context only. But there's nothing in the scope to say that it shouldn't be used on a social/family/home care situation, and there's no reason why a patient or their carer couldn't mitigate a detected issue

Please add Patient and RelatedPerson to this element

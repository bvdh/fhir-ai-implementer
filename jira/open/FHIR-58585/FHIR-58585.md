- **Resolution Description**: The two statements are about different elements. The dosageInstruction element of MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense and MedicationStatement is a single DosageDetails (0..1). Within it, renderedInstruction was 1..1 in the ballot (FHIR-54014) and is 0..1 following FHIR-58907.
- **Description**: 6. https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot5/dosage.html#14.7.0 The "Note to Balloters" states that dosageInstruction is an element in DosageDetails with the cardinality of (0..1). However, the DosageDetails (https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot5/dosage.html#DosageDetails) resource has 'renderedInstruction' cardinality to be [1..1]. Which one is the correct specification?

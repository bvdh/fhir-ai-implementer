- **Description**: `TestScript.setup.action.operation.type` has cardinality `0..1`, although it describes the semantic interaction or operation that is being performed.

When `type` is absent, the intended operation has to be inferred from a combination of fields such as `method`, `resource`, `params` and `url`. I have not found a clear example or use case for an operation without `type`.

A leaner and less ambiguous TestScript structure would have one authoritative way to identify the operation, while allowing additional fields for details that cannot be expressed by that identifier.

Could `operation.type` be changed to `1..1`? If operations without a type are intentionally supported, could the intended use case and expected engine behavior be documented?

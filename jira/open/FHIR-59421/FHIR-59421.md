- **Description**: Summary: The participant-role value set description is out of date, and the set lacks codes for legal and non-professional roles that CareTeam is explicitly designed to hold.

Artifact: ValueSet participant-role (http://hl7.org/fhir/ValueSet/participant-role), bound (example) to CareTeam.participant.role

Description: The value set description says "Defined as: Healthcare professional (occupation) or Services (qualifier value)". The compose actually includes those two SNOMED CT hierarchies plus three individual concepts (patient, caregiver, patient advocate). There are no codes for legal guardian, substitute decision maker or holder of power of attorney, even though CareTeam.participant.supportingInfo is defined specifically to hold "documentation that establishes legal power of attorney or court ordered guardianship". There are also no codes for other common non-professional team roles such as volunteer, peer support worker, or (non-professional) care coordinator. The element definition itself cites "Caregiver" as an example role, so the value set is the only place implementers look for these.

Proposed resolution:

Update the value set description to: "Roles of participants that may be included in a care team, including healthcare professionals, service roles, and non-professional roles such as the patient, family and other caregivers, advocates and legal representatives."
Add SNOMED CT concepts (codes to be confirmed with the terminology facilitator) for:

legal guardian
substitute decision maker / person with power of attorney for health matters
volunteer
peer support worker
care coordinator (not limited to professionals)

If suitable SNOMED CT concepts are not available for some of these, add them to a small FHIR/THO code system and include it.

Impact: Non-substantive (example binding; additive codes and description change).

- **Resolution Description**: We will keep both elements, because they answer different questions. We will clarify their definitions so they do not overlap.

performer is who or what is intended to carry out the administration: the responsible actor. A device is a performer only when it performs the administration itself, for example a closed-loop insulin pump.
device is the equipment to be used for the administration, whether operated by a person or not. Examples: an IV pump programmed by a nurse, a nebulizer, an inhaler spacer, a nasal cannula, a mask.

This matches MedicationAdministration, which has both performer.actor (including Device, "who or what performed the medication administration") and device. It also matches MedicationDispense (performer.actor includes Device) and the Request pattern (Request.performer includes Device). It is not a change from R5.

Changes:

MedicationRequest.performer definition: "The specified desired performer of the medication treatment (e.g. the performer of the medication administration): who or what is intended to carry out the administration. A device is a performer only when it performs the administration itself, for example a closed-loop insulin pump; a device that is used for the administration, such as an IV pump operated by a practitioner, is recorded in device."

This removes the sentences "For devices, this is the device that is intended to perform the administration of the medication. An IV Pump would be an example of a device that is performing the administration. Both the IV Pump and the practitioner that set the rate or bolus on the pump can be listed as performers."

MedicationRequest.device definition: "The device that is intended to be used for the administration of the medication, either the type of device or a specific device, for example an IV pump, a PCA pump, a nebulizer or a nasal cannula, whether operated by a person or not. A device that performs the administration itself is recorded in performer."
- **Description**: Both performer and {{device }}elements can specify a DeviceDefinition for administration.

performer: Can reference DeviceDefinition - "For devices, this is the device that is intended to perform the administration"
device: Can reference DeviceDefinition - "The intended type of device that is to be used for the administration"

The spec even says in performer definition: "An IV Pump would be an example of a device that is performing the administration" - suggesting performer.

But an IV pump for medication administration can go in performer because it "performs" the administration and/or device because it's the "device for administration"?

Thus both elements can specify a DeviceDefinition for administration.

Proposal:

1) remove .device or

2) remove DeviceDefinition as the .performer target and use .device

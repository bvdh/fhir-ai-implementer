- **Description**: existing wording: This value set includes concept codes for specific substances/pharmaceutical products, allergy or intolerance conditions, and negation/exclusion codes to specify the absence of specific types of allergies or intolerances.

Issue: Allowing negation semantics for this element is incompatible with its stated purpose as identification of a substance responsible for adverse reaction risk. Further, the time of the observation of absence of allerggyIntolerance adverse reaction risk is almost always unknown. Even if relatively recent, it is clinically unacceptable practice to assume that no reaction has occurred in the interval or to allow decision support to act without contemporaneous knowledge.

Allowing negation observation statements changes the semantic type of the resource. An AllergyIntolerance instance is a statement of risk. A negated allergy is a historical observation of absence of risk. Those are different clinical concepts with different decision-support implications.

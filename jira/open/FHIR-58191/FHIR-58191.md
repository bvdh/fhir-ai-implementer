- **Description**: In 6.1.2.2.7 XML digital signature used includes XAdES-T and XAdES-X-L. Suggest to revise XAdES-T to XAdES-B-T and XAdES-L-T to XAdES-B-LTA.

Rationale:

1) XAdES-T and XAdES-X-L are defined in ETSI TS 101 903 later replaced by ETSI EN 319 132 in 2016 which defines XAdES-B-T, XAdES-B-LT and XAdES-B-LTA. XAdES-T and XAdES-X-L are not treated as legacy profiles in the new standard.

2) 6.1.2.2.6 is recommending JAdES-B-LTA for JSON signature. XAdES-X-L is not equivalent to JAdES-B-LTA but JAdES-B-LT. (XAdES-A is equivalent is equivalent to JAdES-B-LTA.).

3) XAdES-B-LTA is preferred compared to XAdES-B-LT (XAdES-X-L) for long term signature as it brings features of adding timestamp for the signature to be valid for longer term when the initial algorithm is no longer secure. This is an important feature as now there's need of Post-quantum migration.

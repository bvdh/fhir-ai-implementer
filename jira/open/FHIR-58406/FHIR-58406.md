- **Description**: Procedure.focalDevice backbone should be renamed and allow BiologicallyDerivedProducts to be referenced as well. This backbone is used for references that are fundamental to the procedure. Presently implementers need to accomplish this relationship using the Procedure.used but that element appears intended for incidental use and traceability rather than the focus of the Procedure. If your procedure is to implant an organ that is a focal point of the procedure, not incidental use.

- **Resolution Description**: Make the proposed change;

Replace:

"When the triggering condition is no longer present or no longer considered relevant, the DeviceAlert is deactivated, marking the end of the alert lifecycle."

with:

"When the triggering condition is no longer present or no longer considered relevant, the active alert condition ends, marking the end of the DeviceAlert lifecycle."
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: The text states that when the triggering condition is no longer present or relevant, "the DeviceAlert is deactivated." However, DeviceAlert.status does not define a deactivated state. The available status codes are in-progress, completed, entered-in-error, and unknown. The term "deactivated" may therefore be confused with the DeviceAlert lifecycle status or with signal.activationState.

Suggested change:

Replace:

"When the triggering condition is no longer present or no longer considered relevant, the DeviceAlert is deactivated, marking the end of the alert lifecycle."

with:

"When the triggering condition is no longer present or no longer considered relevant, the active alert condition ends, marking the end of the DeviceAlert lifecycle."

- **Description**: ActivityDefinition appears to have some inconsistencies in the resource types supported by subject[x], product[x], and participant. Could CDS/FHIR-I review and rationalize these, using BiologicallyDerivedProduct (BDP) and Specimen as test cases?

In particular:

subject[x]: Consider whether BiologicallyDerivedProduct, Specimen, and/or SpecimenDefinition should be allowed. An ActivityDefinition may describe an activity performed on a physical product or specimen, for example processing or manipulation of a BDP. The current choices also include several definitional product resources, so it would be useful to clarify the intended semantics for instance versus definitional resources here.

product[x]: Consider adding BiologicallyDerivedProduct to productReference. A BDP can clearly be the product intended to be administered or supplied by an activity. This may be simple enough to address as a separate change if preferred.

participant.typeReference: BDP and Specimen are already permitted, along with several other product/material resource types. However, the terminology available in participant.type does not appear to represent several of these permitted reference types. participant.role and participant.function may also be worth checking for consistency.

The intent is not necessarily for these elements to support the same resource types, but to confirm that the differences are deliberate and that the resource types and associated participant terminology are semantically aligned.

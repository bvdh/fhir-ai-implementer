- **Grouping**: Ready_to_Apply
- **Resolution Description**: Change the definition from
"An explanation as to why this task is held, failed, was refused, etc."
to
"An explanation of the current status of the Task (depending on a combination of Task.status, Task.businessStatus, Task.input, and Task.output)"

Change the comments in task.statusReason from
"This applies to the current status. Look at the history of the task to see reasons for past statuses."
to
"This applies to the current state, which is dependent on a combination of Task.status, Task.businessStatus, Task.input, and Task.output (when there are values for the optional fields)."

- **Description**: 1. Change the definition from

to
An explanation of the current status of the Task (expressed as a combination of Task.status, Task,businessStatus, Task.input, and Task.output)
2. Change the comments from
This applies to the current status. Look at the history of the task to see reasons for past statuses.
to
This applies to the current state, which is expressed as a combintaiton of Task.status, Task.businessStatus, Task.input, and Task.output (when there are values for the optional fields).

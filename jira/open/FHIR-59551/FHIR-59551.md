- **Description**: the probabilityDecimal holds the probability as a percentage in a decimal form. The probabilityQuantity also holds the probability but in a % form.

This means we have two elements that hold the exact same information, but in slightly different form. It feels like a duplication purely to remove the need to convert the decimal into % with the effect of systems having to search two elements to find the same information.

Suggestion: Remove the probabilityQuantity and have systems convert the probabilityDecimal to a % if needed

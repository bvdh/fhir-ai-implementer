- **Resolution Description**: There's already an artifact-topic extension with a context of Questionnaire for this purpose. However, we will:
1. Add this extension to the SearchQuestionnaire SDC profile as mustSupport to increase awareness of it

2. Define a 'standard' search parameter for this extension in the extensions pack (seeing as the primary purpose of having keywords is to be able to search by them)
- **Resolution Vote**: Brian Postlethwaite/Paul Lynch: 4-0-0

- **Change Category**: Enhancement
- **Change Impact**: Compatible, substantive
- **Description**: Questionnaire would benefit from a consistent way to represent topics or keywords for discovery and filtering. Consider adding a topic, keyword or general classification element with an appropriate search parameter. Its naming and semantics could be aligned with comparable elements such as `ResearchStudy.keyword`, `Library.topic` and `Composition.classification`.

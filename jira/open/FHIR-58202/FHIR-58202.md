- **Related Section(s)**: 16.9.4
- **Description**: Sometimes on a bulk payment, additional payments or adjustments are made. For example, there was a period of time when Medicare withheld a specific percentage of all payments (budget balancing activity). There are also occasions when a payment is delayed and the payer pays interest on the payment. (In the X12 835, this is similar to the PLB segment/loop). I don't see a way to handle that with the current PaymentReconciliation.

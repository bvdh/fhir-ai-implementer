- **Description**: The guidance for AllergyIntolerance suggests that its scope is limited to Allery and Intolerance. There are other conditions that present with similar symptoms but are neither, for example Non-allergic hypersensitivity reaction (SCT http://snomed.info/sct 609406000). Provide explicit guidance in the scope statement about these conditions that are neither allergies nor intolerances.

Maybe the scope is intended to be wider than allergies and intolerances, if so, that is not obvious enough.

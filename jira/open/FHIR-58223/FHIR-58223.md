- **Resolution Description**: Will move "However, once a QuestionnaireResponse is marked as 'completed' (including if it is subsequently changed to 'amended'), all requirements of the associated Questionnaire must be met, all non-answered question items must be removed, all non-enabled items must be removed, etc. (Display items MAY be retained. Certain extensions on the Questionnaire item may mandate that a display item needed for a human reviewer to interpret the QuestionnaireResponse be retained in a completed QuestionnaireResponse.)" to a separate paragraph and will change it to:

"However, once a QuestionnaireResponse is marked as 'completed' (including if it is subsequently changed to 'amended'), extraneous information must be removed. Specifically:

All question items with no answer elements must be removed
All group items with no child items must be removed
All non-enabled items (whether through `enableWhen`, [enableWhen-expression]() or some other extension) must be removed.

NOTE: It may be necessary to iterate the above process as removing a group or question might cause a group that had previously not been empty to now be empty. Display items MAY be retained. Certain extensions on the Questionnaire item may mandate that a display item needed for a human reviewer to interpret the QuestionnaireResponse be retained in a completed QuestionnaireResponse.

As well, all requirements of the associated Questionnaire must be met. This includes cardinality constraints, restrictions on content, length, relationships, etc. - both those defined through core elements as well as those defined through extensions. If a form filler doesn't support certain extensions that affect form validity, there is always the potential of non-valid responses being marked as completed. Therefore QuestionnaireResponse consuming systems that dependent on validity against extensions SHOULD perform their own validation steps. See also [sdc-rendering-criticalExtension](StructureDefinition-sdc-rendering-criticalExtension.html) for a mechanism of ensuring that QuestionnaireResponses are only authored by systems that support specific extensions."
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: The validity rules – beyond "must meet data requirements of the Questionnaire" – in the sentence "However, once a QuestionnaireResponse is marked as 'completed' (including if it is subsequently changed to 'amended'), all requirements of the associated Questionnaire must be met, all non-answered question items must be removed, all non-enabled items must be removed, etc." are maybe a little less clear than they might be.

Specifically:

Can "non-answered question items" be slightly restated to e.g. "question items with 0 answers"?
Assuming "non-enabled" covers not just the base "enableWhen", but also use of the "enableWhenExpression" extensions, can that be qualified?
Can "etc." be expanded? E.g., is there an implication that any group items with no sub-items must also be removed?

Alternatively, if some of these and other QuestionnaireResponse validation rules could be stated more formally (e.g. in FHIRPath), that might be helpful to implementors. (This may not be possible across the board, particularly if extensions in implementation guides potentially modify the meaning of "enabled".)

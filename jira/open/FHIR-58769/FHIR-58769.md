- **Description**: Sometimes the content pointed to or contained within an attachment may be expressed in multiple languages. It's too late to change the cardinality on Attachment.language, so the best we can do is define an extension. We should reference the extension from the Attachment.language comment, along with guidance that says that the 'primary' language should be communicated via Attachment.language and if there is no primary, just pick one.

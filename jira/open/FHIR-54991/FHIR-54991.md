- **Resolution Description**: Route and method are still available, in Dosage.route and Dosage.method of each DosageDetails.step.component. They stay at the Dosage level, because they can differ between components (for example, oral plus sublingual as needed), as decided for FHIR-54336 (Not Persuasive, 20 May 2026, 15-0-1).
- **Description**: MedicationStatement.dosageInstruction now uses DosageDetail data type which does include route, and method, etc. Is this no longer needed in MedicationStatement?

- **Description**: Please reconsider the following sentence:

"A diagnostic report is the set of information that is typically provided by a diagnostic service when investigations are complete."

Sometimes reports are issued with a preliminary status when investigations are still not complete, but the partial results are useful or requested by referring physicians. Those are still diagnostic reports and are allowed to be captured in this resource.

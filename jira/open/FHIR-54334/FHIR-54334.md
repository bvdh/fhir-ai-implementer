- **Resolution Description**: The structure of Example 6 is correct: Timing.repeat.count is "a total count of the desired number of repetitions across the duration of the entire timing specification", so count = 5 with Mon/Wed/Fri means five administrations in total (Mon, Wed, Fri, Mon, Wed). We will:

Change the instruction text to: "Iron sucrose: Mon/Wed/Fri at 09:00, 5 doses in total (Mon, Wed, Fri, Mon, Wed)." This also fixes the unmatched quotation mark.
Add to the Structure tab: "count is the total number of administrations, not the number of times the weekly pattern repeats."
Add the dose (1 dose) to the YAML and JSON tabs so that all three representations match the XML.
- **Description**: In example 6 (14.7.1.7) the dosage instruction says "Iron sucrose: 'Mon/Wed/Fri 09:00 × 5 doses total'".

What does '5 doses' mean? Is it five occurences in total? Meaning on Monday, Wednesday, Friday then Monday and Wednesday in the next week? Or five doses every day?

When looking to the structure of the example it could be interpretated as repeat "mon/wed/fr at 09:00" five times, which makes it a total of 15 doses and not 5 doses. 5 doses is not the same as "repeat it for 5 times".

Suggestion is to adjust example 6.

### 3. LauriRondolo - 2026-04-29

costateixeira ,

I think I've always misunderstood the definition of `count` and didn't know that it's actually meant for occurrences and not repetitions. In the comments it says "If you have both bounds and count, then this should be understood as within the bounds period, until count times happens.". I've read this as "only when you have both bounds and count, this should be understood as until count times happen, whenever bounds is not present it's meant for repetitions".

You've mentioned during the virtual WGM that changing Timing wasn't an option but I think changing the phrasing of `count` to something like "A total count of the desired number of occurrences across the entire schedule." would prevent any future misinterpretation.

Considering everything, we propose to change the dosage instruction to say "Iron sucrose: 'Mon/Wed/Fri 09:00 - until 5 have been taken/given" to make it clear and it will match the wording of the last example of Timing https://build.fhir.org/datatypes.html#timing which is "Take every second day, in the morning, until 20 have been taken".

- **Description**: Fields: ActivityDefinition.participant.role, PlanDefinition.actor.option.role, PlanDefinition.action.participant.role, RequestOrchestration.action.participant.role.
All shorts list “Nurse, Surgeon, Parent”, with “E.g.”; ActivityDefinition and RequestOrchestration also add “etc”. These fit the definition; this is coding uncertainty, not a definition conflict. The example binding is http://terminology.hl7.org/ValueSet/action-participant-role. Demonstrated members include practitioner-role nurse (Nurse), pharmacist (Pharmacist), and SNOMED CT 304292004 (Surgeon); membership evidence is open-ended.
Retain these sensible marked examples. Validate Parent coding if presenting coded illustrations, or distinguish it from demonstrated members. Do not substitute guardian, caregiver, or next of kin.

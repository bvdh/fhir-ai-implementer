- **Resolution Description**: No core element is added. The existing dosage-minimumGapBetweenDose extension on Dosage (Duration: "The minimum amount of time that must pass between administering the specified dose amount") expresses "at least 6 hours apart", independently of the dose limit in DosageSafety. We will mention the extension in the DosageSafety section of the Dosage page.
- **Description**: DosageSafety in R6 allows expressing dose limits through doseLimit, including limits per administration or period. However, it does not provide a way to express a minimum interval between administrations.

Example:
Take as needed, at least 6 hours apart, maximum 4 tablets per day.
The maximum daily dose can be represented using doseLimit, but the minimum 6-hour interval cannot be represented independently.

Please consider adding a minimum administration interval to DosageSafety, e.g. as a Duration, so that minimum intervals and dose limits can be expressed independently.

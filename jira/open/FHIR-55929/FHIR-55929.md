- **Resolution Description**: Agreed, clarify the boundaries of DetectedIssue by:

1. Add to the end of the resource introduction the sentence:

In other words, a detected issue is intended to represent an issue that clinical staff could take action to mitigate or prevent.

2. Add to the end of the Boundaries and Relationships section the sentence:

In addition, the DetectedIssue resource is not used to capture complications such as patient reaction to contrast media injected as part of a procedure, that would be captured using Procedure.complication and AdverseEvent. In this case, DetectedIssue would be used prior to the procedure to indicate that the patient had an allergy to the contrast media to be used.

- **Description**: If, during a CT imaging procedure, the patient had a reaction to the contrast media injected as part of the procedure, I would expect that would be captured in Procedure.complication, and if it was a known allergy that was not caught, also an AdverseEvent.

I would expect DetectedIssue would (hopefully) appear after/during the procedure being ordered and before the procedure being performed, in the case of the allergy being known.

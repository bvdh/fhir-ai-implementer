- **Description**: Request change to the standard IG template to include direct link to Open CCO License: https://www.hl7.org/fhir/license.html#license

Health regulators often ask if FHIR is free or whether there is a license maintenance cost. It might help to present a more prominent link to the CCO license. For instance, adding a direct link to it in the IG footer template, and on the main Welcome to FHIR homepage.

Applicable to FHIR R5 and R6.

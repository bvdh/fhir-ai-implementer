- **Description**: A lot of vital sign observations needs additional information to be useful to clinicians. The additional information is not the "state" of the subject during observation, not related to method, device, bodypart or bodystructure. Some of this additional data points are documented in the OpenEHR Archetypes like the "state" related to Bloodpressure.

Some examples are:

Position
Confounding factors
Exertion
Sleep status
Tilt

We have documented some extensions to deal with this extended content in a proposal for Norwegian domain profiles for vital signs observations. no-domain-extensions

We propose to add official extensions to handle the most important additional datapoints concerning vital sign observations and distribute them with the official vital signs profiles.

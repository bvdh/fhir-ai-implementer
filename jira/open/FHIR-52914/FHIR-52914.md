- **Description**: We want to utilize the ServiceRequest resource for its intended purpose (Advanced Directive), such as CPR, blood transfusion, etc.

In the Netherlands there are agreementparties required to display when this Advance Directive will requested during emergency care cases:

Required:

HealthProfessional (PractitionerRole)

At least one is required of this option:

Representative::ContactPerson (ReleatedPerson) - more is possible
Patiënt

So I'm interested for this information (references), like an agreementparties element, could be added in Servicerequest.

P.S. The LivingWill document like POLST from US and other information from the agreement will be noticed in the Consent resource and we will use the ServiceRequest.supportingInfo to make the reference.

- **Description**: The comments on `ResearchStudy.objective.outcomeMeasure.intervention` and `ResearchStudy.objective.outcomeMeasure.comparator` suggest including a `Group` in `ResearchStudy.contained` and referencing it by its internal id to reuse a group referenced by `ResearchStudy.comparisonGroup.eligibility`.

It is unclear why the comments specifically highlight containment. Since these elements are of type `Reference(Group)`, the same `Group` could also be reused through an external literal reference or a logical reference using `Reference.identifier`.

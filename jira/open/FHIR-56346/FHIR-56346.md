- **Description**: Current definition of 'outpatient' ("Includes requests for medications to be administered or consumed in an outpatient setting") seems vague, as a 'setting' is not necessarily a care provision facility, and this term seems to have significant overlap with 'community'. The examples point in the right direction, but they're just examples. I suggest "Includes requests for medications to be administered or consumed in an ambulatory facility".

Likewise, in the definition of 'community', "in their home" seems excessively restrictive, and the examples suggest necessary involvement of health care personnel. I propose "Includes instructions or approvals for medications to be dispensed and administered or consumed by the patient outside of the prescribing facility". The mention of nursing homes & hospice complicates things: recommend clarification that this is not a restatement but an extension by stating "(this would also include . . .)".

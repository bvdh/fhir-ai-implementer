- **Description**: The MedicationDispense definition assumes a prescription. The resource definition says it is "for a named person/patient… The medication dispense is the result of a pharmacy system responding to a medication order." The introduction softens this to "usually in response to a prescription". In Nigeria most supply at community pharmacies and patent and proprietary medicine vendors (PPMVs) happens without a prescription. The model allows that, but the definition says it's out of scope.

We should fix this - it seems to be the intent that it could cover prescription-less dispense

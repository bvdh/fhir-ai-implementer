### 6. Yanick Gaudet - 2025-11-14

Finnie Flores: Here are some info. Hopefully these help...

There is ongoing work in Alberta to develop clinical, patient, and referral pathways ([Alberta's Pathway Hub | Alberta Health Services|https://www.albertahealthservices.ca/aph/Page18236.aspx]). Among the pathways that have been developed so far are gastroenterology referral pathways for colorectal cancer (high-risk). These pathways ask the referring provider to perform certain investigations and, based on the results, to make an "urgent" or "semi-urgent" referral for colonoscopy or other examination procedure. The "urgent" and "semi-urgent" values are associated with specific timelines within which those procedures should be performed. Based on the published information, low-risk pathways are also being developed; I would guess that these might include "routine" referrals for those procedures.

These specific pathways are Iron Deficiency Anemia (IDA) Pathway for Colorectal Cancer Diagnosis and High Risk Rectal Bleeding Pathway for Colorectal Cancer (CRC) Diagnosis.

You will see the "semi-urgent" priority on those two specific pathways.

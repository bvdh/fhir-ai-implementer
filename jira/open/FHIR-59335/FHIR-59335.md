- **Resolution Description**: 1. Add a steward element to the canonical resource pattern:

Name: steward 0..* ContactDetail
Definition: The name of the organization or individual that has delegated responsibility for the ongoing maintenance of the [[title]].
Comment:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that authors or publishes the content. The steward is the primary point of contact for questions or issues with the [[title]]. This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.

2. Update the publisher element in the canonical resource pattern:

a. short:
From:
Name of the publisher/steward (organization or individual)
To:
Name of the publisher (organization or individual)

b. definition:
From:
The name of the organization or individual responsible for the release and ongoing maintenance of the value set.
To:
The name of the organization or individual that makes the [[title]] available and is responsible for the release and maintenance of the [[title]].

c. comment:
From:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that developed and initially authored the content. The publisher is the primary point of contact for questions or issues with the [[title]]. This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.
To:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that developed and initially authored the content. The publisher is typically the primary point of contact for questions or issues with the [[title]], but may also delegate this responsibility to steward(s). This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.

Propagate these changes to all canonical resources in the specification.
- **Description**: CanonicalResource currently defines publisher, which has historically been a synonym for steward in the FHIR space. However, multiple communities have a need to represent the concept of a steward and a publisher because they may be different.

To allow this distinction, we propose to:

1. Add a steward element to the canonical resource pattern:

Name: steward 0..1 ContactDetail
Definition: The name of the organization or individual that is responsible for the ongoing maintenance of the [[title]].
Comment:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that authors or publishes the content. The steward is the primary point of contact for questions or issues with the [[title]]. This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.

2. Update the publisher element in the canonical resource pattern:

a. short:
From:
Name of the publisher/steward (organization or individual)
To:
Name of the publisher (organization or individual)

b. definition:
From:
The name of the organization or individual responsible for the release and ongoing maintenance of the value set.
To:
The name of the organization or individual that makes the [[title]] available.

c. comment:
From:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that developed and initially authored the content. The publisher is the primary point of contact for questions or issues with the [[title]]. This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.
To:
Usually an organization but may be an individual. This is not necessarily the same individual or organization that is responsible for the content. This item SHOULD be populated unless the information is available from context.

Propagate these changes to all canonical resources in the specification.

History:
Note that the publisher element was updated to clarify that it was intended as a synonym of steward, but this has caused difficulty in various communities because many consumers see publisher and steward as discrete concepts. See FHIR-29944 and FHIR-35987.

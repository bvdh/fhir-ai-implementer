- **Description**: Summary: CareTeam.reason allows only Condition references; care teams are often formed for reasons recorded as observations or assessments, particularly in social care.

Element: CareTeam.reason (0..* CodeableReference(Condition))

Description: Care teams in community and social care are frequently established in response to assessed needs or risks rather than diagnoses: social isolation, carer strain or breakdown, frailty, falls risk, safeguarding concerns. These are commonly recorded as Observations (including SDOH and assessment results). EpisodeOfCare.reason.value already allows Condition | DiagnosticReport | HealthcareService | Observation | Procedure. CareTeam.reason cannot reference any of these other than Condition, so the reason must be coded without a link to the supporting record.

Proposed resolution:

Widen CareTeam.reason targets to Condition | Observation | DiagnosticReport | Procedure (aligned with EpisodeOfCare.reason.value, excluding HealthcareService).
Definition: "Describes why the care team exists. This may be a health condition, or an assessed need, risk or social circumstance."

Impact: Compatible, substantive (target widening).

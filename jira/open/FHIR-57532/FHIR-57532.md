- **Description**: Need to create a bundle that contains a DiagnosticReport and the resources that DiagnosticReport references to encode key information such as (in the case of an imaging report) the Patient, ServiceRequest (order), Procedure, ImagingStudy, Observations (so many observations), ImagingSelections, Attachments, and Communications. E.g. this bundle might be conveyed from an imaging center to a hospital down the street, or from a hospital in one country to a hospital in another country.

The DiagnosticReport might or might not include references to one or more .presentedForm which provide additional human-readable presentations.

The DiagnosticReport might or might not include a reference to a .composition to provide another human-readable presentation.

The bundle needs to be signable (via Provenance) by the clinical creator when needed to attest to the clinical validity of the content.

The bundle should be understood to have persistence as a clinical document.

Option 1:

Revise Bundle.type “document” requirements to allow the first entry in a Bundle to be either Composition or DiagnosticReport.

Keep all the associated behaviors as is.

Option 2:

Add a new Bundle.type “report” (or something similar).

Replicate all the “document” behaviors into report.

Allow the first entry in a Bundle to be either Composition or DiagnosticReport

Option 3:

Keep constraint that Bundle.type "document" is prohibited from having anything other than a Composition as the first entry.

Modify Composition to function as a wrapper for DiagnosticReport to meet the document Bundle.type constraint. (See separate JIRA)

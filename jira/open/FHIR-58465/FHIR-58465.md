- **Description**: The current constraint prevents collection.collector from being present whenever collection.procedure is present.
However, Procedure.performer is 0..*, so a Procedure may be recorded without identifying who performed it. In this case, collection.collector may still be needed to identify the person or organization that collected the specimen.
Consider allowing both elements when the Procedure does not identify a performer.

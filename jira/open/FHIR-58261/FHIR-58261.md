- **Description**: In R6, left and right are no longer included in the value set for BodyStructure.includedStructure.qualifier.

What was the rationale for removing them?

This may leave a modelling gap for left- or right-sided regions of non-lateralizable structures, e.g. the left wall of the urinary bladder. lateral is not equivalent to left, while laterality = left may not be appropriate if the selected structure itself is not lateralizable.

Should left and right be available as qualifier values for such cases, or is the intent that these must always be represented using laterality and/or a more specific structure concept?

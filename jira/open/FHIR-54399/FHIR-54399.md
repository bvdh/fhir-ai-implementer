Waitlisting the appointment (optional):

This optional step permits creating a waitlisted appointment. This can occur when either no appropriate slot is available, or a time that is not ideal for the patient.

In this scenario, there would be potentially three different Appointment resources:

In the case where a non-preferred slot is booked, there will be a currently booked Appointment, which may be at a far future date.
The waitlist Appointment, which indicates the time range or other properties indicating when a preferable slot would be desired (e.g., a geographically closer location, a earlier date.
In the case where a preferable slot becomes available, there will be a newly booked Appointment.

If there is no available slot, then simply create the appointment with a status of waitlist, and optionally include a requestedPeriod, serviceType, appointmentType, specialty, participants or any other properties that are needed to indicate what the desired appointment should be for. This can happen for services that have limited supply and high demand as they often have long waitlists. Allocation of slots to these type of appointments is often processed based on a triage process that considers various factors.

An Appointment representing a waitlist entry can refer to the booked appointments that may be replaced. For example, if you currently have an appointment scheduled for six months in the future, and have a waitlist Appointment indicating you would prefer an appointment next week, the waitlist Appointment would refer to your six month out appointment via the waitlist-will-replace [[TODO: better name?] extension.

If you have a waitlist appointment and are offered a slot sooner than your currently booked appointment, but still not within your preferred timeframe, the currently booked appointment can be cancelled, the offered appointment can be booked, and the waitlist-will-replace [[TODO: better name?] extension on the waitlist appointment may be updated to point to the newly booked appointment. In this case, the Appointment.replaces property on the newly booked appointment would point to the now cancelled future appointment.

e.g. Book an available appointment that is 4 months out, but request a waitlist item to get in earlier if a slot becomes available (due to someone else cancelling).

If new time slots become available during the requestedPeriod, the scheduling system, or staff at the scheduling organization, can notify the patient that a new time slot is available. If the patient chooses, the waitlisted appointment would then be booked into that specific slot, and the previously booked appointment would be canceled. The specific business process for notifying patients of new availability is not specified, and is up to the implementing system to determine. These slots could be before or after the booked appointment, however could be profiled to restrict.

- **Description**: For waitlisted appointments, there should be a way to identify an appointment to replace in case this one occurs. There is a replaces element, that could be used for this, but there is no guidance about it being used for this purpose. In fact the description says it is replacing a cancelled appointment. Some guidance on how to do specify this link should be added so that if the waitlisted appointment is accepted, the original (less desirable) appointment can be cancelled or somehow flagged for manual follow-up.

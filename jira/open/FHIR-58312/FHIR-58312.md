- **Description**: In the text the use of "kind" was replaced with "general class", which is a more descriptive term. Suggest that the Description of DeviceDefinition in the structure is changed from "The definition of a kind of device or device component" to "The definition of a general class of device or device component"

Suggest that this change is applied everywhere.

Alternatively, the switch in DeviceDefinition can be undone and stick with "kind".

- **Resolution Description**: device is still needed. Most devices in a medication order are used for the administration rather than performing it (a nebulizer, a spacer, a nasal cannula, an IV pump operated by a nurse), and those belong in device, not performer. FHIR-54416 clarifies both definitions, so it is clear which element to use. No further change beyond FHIR-54416.

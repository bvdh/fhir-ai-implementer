- **Description**: The rules for ImagingSelection proposed restrictions should be extended

there are constraints for ImagingSelection.instance which only relate to frameNumber, referencedContentItemIdentifier, segmentNumber, regionOfInterest, waveFormChannel to being mutually exclusive. But they do not take into account imageRegion2D. However I think imageRegion2D and referencedContentItemIdentifier, segementNumber, regionOfInterest and waveFormChannel are mutually exclusive. So I recommend to add another rule
Is another rule needed that frameOfReferenceUid requires a studyUid to be present? Otherwise it may be hard to access the image data associated with a frame of reference UID (e.g. there is no resource in the DICOM Studies webservice for a Frame Of Reference)

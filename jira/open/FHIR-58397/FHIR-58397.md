- **Resolution Description**: We will move `MedicationStatement.relatedClinicalInformation` to directly after `MedicationStatement.reason` (it is currently after `note`). Placing it right after `medication` would separate `medication` from `subject`, `encounter` and `effective[x]`. Placing it next to `reason` keeps the two related elements together, as the definition of relatedClinicalInformation already contrasts it with `reason`. The resulting order is: ... `derivedFrom`, `reason`, `relatedClinicalInformation`, `note`, `dosage`, `adherence`.
- **Change Category**: Enhancement
- **Description**: Could relatedClinicalInformation be stiuated right after medication? It is pretty close to being a medication reason so it would make sense for it to be closer to the medication element.

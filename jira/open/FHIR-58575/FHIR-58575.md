- **Description**: `DocumentReference` would benefit from a standard element for representing topics or keywords used for document discovery and filtering.

`DocumentReference.category` categorizes the type of document, while elements such as `event`, `practiceSetting` and `bodyStructure` represent more specific clinical metadata. None provides a general and clearly defined representation of the document’s subject matter or keywords.

Please consider adding a topic, keyword or general classification element together with an appropriate search parameter. Its naming and semantics should be aligned with comparable elements such as `ResearchStudy.keyword`, `Library.topic` and `Composition.classification`.

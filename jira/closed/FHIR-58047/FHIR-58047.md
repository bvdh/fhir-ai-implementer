- **Description**: CarePlan.addresses doesn't allow a reference to an Observation resource. In clinical practice, findings sometimes trigger plans of care, e.g. a lung nodule found in an X-Ray (just a finding, not yet a diagnosis/condition) will trigger a CT scan request, and an appointment with the pulmonologist.

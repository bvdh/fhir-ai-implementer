- **Resolution Description**: Change the text summary parameters for ResearchStudy.comparisonGroup to:

Short: A cohort of study participants that is compared to another group

Definition: Describes a group of subjects (participants) in a study that is compared to another group. This group may be an arm of the study, such as controlled trials that define groups for assignment into specific paths or protocols involving different exposures. This group may also be defined for other types of comparisons, such as descriptive or predictive studies characterizing populations by their characteristics.

### 4. Gustav Vella - 2026-09-02

Analysis

Grahame's complaint is accurate. The redesign isn't wrong, it's just that the short/definition/comment text was never updated when the element's scope grew.

Before R6, arm (now comparisonGroup, still listed under "Alternate Names: arm") described a single thing: the sequence of events/exposures a subject follows (drug A → washout → drug B). That's exactly what the current text still says.

In R6 the element picked up sub-elements that describe something different: The population assigned to that path, not the path itself: targetNumber/actualNumber (planned vs. actual enrollment), eligibility (Reference(Group) - inclusion/exclusion criteria for this subset), and observedGroup (Reference(Group) - who actually ended up in it).

Meanwhile description and intendedExposure still carry the "path/sequence of events" content that used to be the whole element.

So comparisonGroup is now doing double duty: it's both the definition of a pathway and the definition of a cohort of subjects assigned to that pathway, with enrollment and eligibility tracking. That's a legitimate and arguably necessary broadening e.g. results-reporting registries (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov) need target vs. actual accrual and eligibility per arm/cohort, which the old "just a sequence of events" model had no home for. The bug is purely editorial: the short/definition/comment weren't widened to match.

Suggested disposition

Keep the redesign and fix the text. Proposed replacement:

Short: "A defined group of subjects following a comparable path through the study"

Definition: "Describes a group of subjects assigned to a comparable, defined path through the study (e.g., an arm in a clinical trial, or a comparable set of specimens in a non-clinical/stability study). Captures the group's defining path (see .description, .intendedExposure), its eligibility criteria (.eligibility), and its enrollment (.targetNumber, .actualNumber, .observedGroup)."

Comment: "In many clinical trials this is referred to as the ARM (or Cohort) of the study, though the term is not used universally even where a comparison between groups exists. Prior to R6 this element represented only the sequence of events/exposures defining the path; it has since been extended to also represent the population assigned to that path, to support eligibility and enrollment (target vs. actual) reporting."

Also worth flagging in the disposition: "Alternate Names" could add "cohort" alongside "arm," since non-trial study types (e.g. stability studies) use that language and the element now explicitly supports non-clinical-trial use cases via its own examples.

### 9. Andrea Pitkus - 2025-10-16

Note there are two places where LOINC is used in FHIR Diagnostic Report:

In FHIR Diagnostic Report, the LOINC Document Ontology codes indicate the type of report/document.
In the FHIR Observation Resource, LOINC codes for the different types of Observations in the report, whether laboratory, pathology, radiology, vital signs, clinical surveys, etc. would be used corresponding to each Observation.

- **Description**: FHIR-39656, whose title was "Search parameter for finding ObservationDefinitions linked to ResearchStudies", requested:

1) The ObservationDefinition be added to the context of use on https://build.fhir.org/extension-workflow-researchstudy.html

2) A search parameter be created for finding ObservationDefinitions linked to a ResearchStudy.

The tracker item was approved without changes, applied, and published, but #1 was not done, and while a SearchParameter was created for finding resources linked to a research study (https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/fhir-extensions/SearchParameter-workflow-researchStudy.html), ObservationDefintiion is not in the list of resources on that page.

Please add ObservationDefinition to both https://build.fhir.org/extension-workflow-researchstudy.html and https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/fhir-extensions/SearchParameter-workflow-researchStudy.html.

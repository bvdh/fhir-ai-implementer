The representation of Draft (FMM 0) is often confusing to those new to FHIR and require them to ask to verify the intent. FMM 0 is never specifically defined or attributed to Draft - it is only implied. The FMM Levels table begins with "Draft(0) the artifact has been published on..." Then FMM 1 is defined, referencing FMM 0 and not Draft: "FMM 1 FMM0 + the artifact produces no warnings...". There should be a more clear declaration of the FMM 0 <--> Draft relationship.

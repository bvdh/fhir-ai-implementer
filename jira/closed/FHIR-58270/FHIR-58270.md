Suggested change
Mark affected Observation records using the appropriate correction specified in Observation.status. Use amended or corrected when the observation remains part of the clinical record but has been superseded. Use entered-in-error only when the Observation should no longer be considered a valid clinical record. Also consider using Observation.statusReason, Provenance, or implementation-guide specific mechanisms to indicate suspected device-related quality concerns.

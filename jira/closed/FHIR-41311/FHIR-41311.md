- **Resolution Description**: The Availability data type will be updated to add new property to Availability.availableTime to represent the general time period for which the available time applies.

Availability.availableTime.period 0..1 Period

Display: When the availability applies

Definition: The period of time when the availability is applicable. For example, you might use this property to indicate the period during the holiday season when you close an hour early.

Comment: The parent resource may have a period that applies to the overall lifecycle of the resource. In those cases, the Availability.availableTime.period property may be considered a more specific description of when that resource is available. Thus it would normally be expected that the Availability.availableTime.period is within the period specified by the containing resource.

- **Description**: Update the availability datatype to include a period for each availability block, and also provide guidance on how the resource period and the availability period interact.

Specifically that the resource period provide the overall available from until periods and those within the datatype only apply to that specific block - permitting changing hours general hours over different periods of time.

Also consider if the appointment recurrence-template content would be appropriate to include here also, or if that's more appropriate as extensions/modifier extensions.

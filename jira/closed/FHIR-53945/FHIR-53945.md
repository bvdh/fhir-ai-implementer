- **Resolution Description**: Move this section to Questionnaire with the exception of the sentence "When working with QuestionnaireResponses, there may be FHIRPath expressions defined on the associated Questionnaire that will be evaluated in the context of the QuestionnaireResponse."
As part of that move, change "The following additional guidance applies to using FHIRPath with QuestionnaireResponses" to "The following additional guidance applies to using [FHIRPath](link to FHIR FHIRPath page) with Questionnaires and QuestionnaireResponses".
Will place the "When working with QuestionnaireResponses, there may be FHIRPath [expressions(link to FHIRpath guidance in Questionnaire) defined on the associated Questionnaire that will be evaluated in the context of the QuestionnaireResponse." sentence in the QuestionnaireResponse intro as a separate paragraph following the sentence "QuestionnaireResponse resources can be validated against their corresponding Questionnaire to verify that required groups and questions are answered and that answers fit constraints in terms of cardinality, data type, etc."
Will update the word [validated] to point to the section on validation in the notes on the same page.

- **Description**: The FHIRPath additions page is just as valid on the Questionnaire page, as on the QR page.

Alternately move this section to Questionnaire, and create a summary here on QR referring to the moved content.

- **Resolution Description**: Given R6 being normative and the impact that constraints have when validating resources, and the possible exceptions (e.g. when an element is populated with a DAR) it seems risky to add even more constraints especially if they are rules.

We will lower the level of mdd-1 to a warning, because we imagine that in some cases an implementer may interpret "handedOver" to be before "prepared" if there are unusual cases for preparing medications or legacy data that needs to be exchanged.

- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Compatible, substantive
- **Description**: Currently, there is a Constraint mdd-1: "whenHandedOver cannot be before whenPrepared."

But there's no constraint relating these timestamps to status:

status = cancelled, but whenHandedOver is populated?
status = declined should have whenPrepared but not whenHandedOver?

Propose adding Status-timestamp invariants like:

{{"whenHandedOver }}only if status = preparation | in-progress | cancelled | on-hold | completed | entered-in-error | unfulfilled | declined | unknown"

Create a new Jira to handle the following recommendations in a future IG (or future version of existing):

provide guidance for establishment of "device list" modeled parallel to US Core medication list as noted

As part of the DAM we are defining types of lists that are relevant and then as part of the UDI IG, will identify how to implement each one.
The list would include:

Devices that have been implanted, explanted or inactivated in a patient
Devices used on a patient during an encounter (e.g., inpatient, outpatient) Includes Implants and all other devices
Devices used on a cohort (group) during a specified treatment protocol
Devices related to a patient safety event (e.g., infections)
Devices related to a procedure type
Devices involved in laboratory (diagnostics) test for a specific indication

Alternatively, defer to ServiceRequest and model the "device list" using ServiceRequest which has the required elements.

- **Description**: DeviceUseStatement is currently at maturity level = 0 without sufficient clinical use cases to support it. There are potential use cases for a monitoring device (e.g., blood pressure monitor or CPAP device) to provide a set of findings based on usage but some of these device manufacturers have not expressed interest in using standards to allow interoperability as compared with proprietary configuration. However, the expectation that a patient would use a structure such as DeviceUseStatement to indicate use of a cane, wheelchair or other assistive device seems like excessive overhead to report an observation.

I propose that DeviceRequest follow the pattern that US Core established in its latest update to reference an active medication list; I.e., use MedicationRequest (status = active, intent = order and indicate requester to determine if an actual prescription <organization>, reported by doctor <practitioner>, or reported by patient or caregiver <patient, relatedPerson>). The result is a list that supports how existing EHRs handle medication lists.

Feedback from vendors suggests that other aspects (including device use) is handled in a similar way to suggest the wheelchair was ordered as DME, or reported by a physician that someone else prescribed it outside the organization, or that the patient obtained his/her own in some way. The parallel to medication list to create a "device list" would be:

DeviceRequest.status = active
DeviceRequest.intent = order
DeviceRequest.requester (see comments below):
DeviceRequest.requester – MedicationRequest.requester includes organization, practitioner, patient, relatedPerson as options for type. DeviceRequest.requester only includes types as organization, practitioner, and device. Therefore, one could not include patient or relatedPerson as a requester to follow the pattern used for Medication list.
DeviceRequest.performer (requested filler) – does allow use of practitioner, organization, careteam, patient, device or relatedPerson – Thus, performer might work even though not exactly parallel to Medication list modeling

Hence, DeviceRequest.requester needs to allow options of patient and relatedPerson as does MedicationRequest and ServiceRequest.

Bottom line recommendation:

provide guidance for establishment of "device list" modeled parallel to US Core medication list as noted
add "patient" and "relatedPerson" as options for DeviceRequest.requester to accommodate documentation of devices obtained by and used by a patient without order by a practitioner or organization.
Alternatively, defer to ServiceRequest and model the "device list" using ServiceRequest which has the required elements.

### 7. Hans Buitendijk - 2020-03-02

Like the Medication List project, which is to provide guidance on how to create various medication lists, including what Argonaut has been working in, likely DeviceRequest will be a main source for a device list of sorts. However, like medication lists, DeviceUseStatement may be a source as well. Or perhaps DeviceDispense (a proposal is in flight).

However, it is not accurate to only look at Observations for device use. Sure, an Observation has the ability to record the device used to make the observation. But the general statement that I'm using this or that device would still be DeviceUseStatement. As a Device has a reference to a patient, you could argue that may be enough and other use statements that would need more than that yet would not fit with an Observation using a device is not a large space. If so, DeviceUseStatement will not go much beyond FMM1 in reality. But that space does not look appropriate to just be filled by Observations as that is flipping the perspective from what device is used in for various purposes to what device is used to make the observation. Those are two different perspectives.

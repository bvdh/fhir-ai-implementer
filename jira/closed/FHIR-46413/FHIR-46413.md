- **Resolution Description**: We will create a standard extension (0..*) for Medication to provide characteristcs:

characteristic.type CodeableConcept 1..1
characteristic.value[x] 1..1 boolean, dateTime, decimal, integer, string, CodeableConcept, Quantity, Ratio
- **Resolution Vote**: Stephen Chu / Frank McKinney : 8-0-0
- **Vote Date**: 2025-09-29
- **Change Category**: Enhancement
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: The analysis of national implementations of Medication resource shows that there are very many extensions for very many different national needs.

In order to reduce the amount of needed national extensions a common extension for any characteristics could be added to Medication resource. This would be a key-value pair, similar to what is seen in MedicationKnowledge or MedicinalProductDefinition.

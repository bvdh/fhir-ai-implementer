# FHIR-41407: QuestionnaireResponse should allow for a family to fill out the questionnaire

## Metadata

- **Summary**: QuestionnaireResponse should allow for a family to fill out the questionnaire
- **Created**: 2023-06-09
- **Updated**: 2025-03-20
- **Resolved**: 2023-10-05
- **Status**: Applied
- **Project**: FHIR Specification Feedback
- **Type**: Change Request
- **Reporter**: Grahame Grieve
- **Resolution**: Persuasive with Modification
- **Specification**: FHIR Core (FHIR)
- **Raised in Version**: R6
- **Work Group**: FHIR Infrastructure
- **Related Artifact(s)**: QuestionnaireResponse
- **Grouping**: SDC-CORE
- **Resolution Description**: We will add Group as an allowed author and source, but will add a comment to both indicating that the referenced Group SHALL be defined as only containing members of type Patient and/or RelatedPerson. In other cases, Organization must be used.
- **Resolution Vote**: Brian Postlethwaite/Paul Lynch: 2-0-0
- **Vote Date**: 2023-10-05
- **Change Category**: Enhancement
- **Change Impact**: Compatible, substantive
- **Description**: In Africa, care is very often provided to and administered for families, not patients, and families are considered to act as a group. Questionnaire is able to have a subject of group, but the author and source can't be family (=group)

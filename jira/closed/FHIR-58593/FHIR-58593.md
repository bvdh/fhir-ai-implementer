- **Resolution Description**: Both corrections were already made when applying FHIR-53977 (PR 4326):

"These should recorded as Social History Observations" → "These should be recorded as Social History Observations".

- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: MedicationStatement, Section 14.4.1, Scope and Usage. The sentence of "These should recorded as Social History Observations", where obviously there is a missing 'be' - "should [be] recorded...".
1.3.1. Two paragraphs below in the same subsection, the sentence of "is a report by a patient, significant other or a clinician that one or more of the prescribe, dispense or administer actions has occurred, resulting is a belief...", where "resulting is" should be "resulting in"

- **Description**: FamilyMemberHistory.condition.onset[x] includes the following choices:
. onset[x] 0..1 When condition first manifested
..... onsetAge Age
..... onsetRange Range
..... onsetPeriod Period
..... onsetString

FamilyMemberHistory is useful from the Public Health context for capturing communicable diseases of family members in contact with the subject.
Knowledge of onsetDate is clinically useful

Suggest adding:
onsetDate to the choice list

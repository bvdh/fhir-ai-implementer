### 3. Andrea Pitkus - 2026-08-20

Would also update the following from "serum or serum/plasma glucose" to "serum glucose" or "plasma glucose" to correctly use the test name that would be ordered. Laboratories typically only have a serum glcuose on their menu or a plasma glucose, not both. Both are NOT performed on the same specimen, even though either individual test order would be mapped to the same LOINC code that indicates serum or plasma. In SNOMED CT there are separate serum glucose codes and plasma glucose codes that better reflect the reality of testing. It is also why the LOINC as a codeable concept cannot be used solely and the actual test name (and other details) for glucose (and many other lab tests) is needed to convey the accurate meaning. Is the glucose test a serum glucose OR is it a plasma glucose OR less commonly a whole blood glucose such as for point of care testing?

In summary we have a dosage we want to send in FHIR where the dosage instruction the clinician wants to replicate in FHIR is:

1 puff twice a day for maintenance. 1 puff as needed to relieve symptoms. Max dose 8 puffs per day.

This needs to be broken down into 2 concurrent (sequence number the same) dosage instructions for the maintenance (patient must take) and the "as needed" (optional for patient to take dependant on symptoms) dosages. However there is the "Max dose of 8 puffs per day" which applies to both dosage instructions.

This is a real world example for [Symbicort Turbohaler 200/6 Inhalation powder - Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) - (emc) | 1327|https://gbr01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.medicines.org.uk%2Femc%2Fproduct%2F1327%2Fsmpc&data=05%7C02%7Cbill.lush%40nhs.net%7Cb19a19a478a548ea4ae808ddb7adf773%7C37c354b285b047f5b22207b48d774ee3%7C0%7C0%7C638868679982769070%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=wn%2FixaOCPMgSC0zbMzuJw9wCU62Fa464Zjs%2BRqDkUjU%3D&reserved=0].

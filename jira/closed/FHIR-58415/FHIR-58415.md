- **Description**: "The kind of structure being represented by the body structure at BodyStructure.location. This can define both normal and abnormal morphologies."
BodyStructure.location is an R4 element that was removed for R5. The definitional text for this element needs to be updated.

Existing Wording:
The kind of structure being represented by the body structure at BodyStructure.location. This can define both normal and abnormal morphologies.

Proposed Wording:
The morphology of the included anatomical or pathological structure. This can define both normal and abnormal morphologies.

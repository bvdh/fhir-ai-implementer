"There are situations where FHIR requires data to be transformed as part of the exchange process and these transformations will potentially cause signature issues unless a custom canonicalization mechanism is chosen that takes the changes into account. These include:

Expectations for translating relative urls into absolute urls or moving referenced data inline in the [Bulk Data Exchange](async-bulk.html#3.2.6.1.5.0.4) portion of the spec"

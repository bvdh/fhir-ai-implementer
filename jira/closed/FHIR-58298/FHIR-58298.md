- **Description**: The resources Condition, Procedure and AllergyIntolerance capture the source of information differently:

Field
Cardinality
Datatype
Definition

Condition
asserter
0..1
Reference(Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Patient | RelatedPerson | Device | Group)
Individual or device that is making the condition statement.

Procedure
reported[x]
0..1
boolean | Reference(Patient | RelatedPerson | Practitioner | PractitionerRole | Organization)
Indicates if this record was captured as a secondary 'reported' record rather than as an original primary source-of-truth record. It may also indicate the source of the report.

AllergyIntolerance
asserter
0..1
Reference(Patient | RelatedPerson | Practitioner | PractitionerRole)
The source of the information about the allergy that is recorded.

Note that asserter is not used consistently either — in Condition it identifies who makes the clinical statement, in AllergyIntolerance it identifies the source of the information. These are different concepts.

Proposed change:

Reconsider the difference between "asserter" and "reported"
If the two concepts are found to be equivalent: rename asserter to reported[x] in Condition and AllergyIntolerance, as in Procedure.
If they are found to be distinct: add reported[x] alongside the existing asserter in both resources.
In either case, the boolean form (reportedBoolean) is required to cover the unknown-source case.
align the permitted target resource types for the Reference

currently in use in every resource: Patient, RelatedPerson, Practitioner, PractitionerRole
add to Condition: Organization
add to Procedure: Device, Group
add to AllergyIntolerance: Device, Group, Organization

Rationale:

There is currently no way to indicate that a Condition or AllergyIntolerance is a secondary record at all — and in particular no way to do so when the original source cannot be identified. asserter cannot express this: it requires a nameable party, and its presence does not distinguish a primary record from a secondhand one.

Concrete implementation need: in Austria a national system for capturing patient diagnoses is currently being designed. A GP will regularly record a diagnosis originating from a third party (reportedReference), and in a relevant share of cases the original source is not identifiable (reportedBoolean = true). Reported data is subject to different editing rules and is expected to be displayed differently from primary data — the same requirement already stated for Procedure.reported[x]. This need is not Austria-specific; it arises in any jurisdiction consolidating diagnoses across provider organisations.

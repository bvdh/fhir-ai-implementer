- **Description**: The first paragraph says "DeviceAssociation records a relationship between a device and a subject (or alternatively a focus entity)". However, Ballot 5 changed DeviceAssociation.subject to 1..1, while focus remains 0..1. Therefore a focus cannot actually be used as an alternative to subject. The same section later correctly says focus can be specified in addition to the subject.

This is an internal contradiction and could cause implementers to believe a DeviceAssociation containing only focus is valid.

Proposed change:

Replace the opening sentence with:

"DeviceAssociation records a relationship between a device and a required subject, with an optional focus entity where additional context is needed."

### 3. Elliot Silver - 2026-08-27

The intent is that the first paragraph be read as "DeviceAssociation records a relationship between a device and a subject, or between a device and a focus entity in the context of a subject." Focus is not intended to be use as an alternative to subject. Subject is the entity in whose record this association is placed, and is usually the target of the relationship. In some cases, even though the association belongs in the subject's record, they aren't the target of the association, and in that case focus is used. A device given to a patient's parent would be listed in the patient's record (subject), but with the related person as the focus.

- **Description**: For sampled data that represents waveforms from medical devices a possibility is needed that allows to annotate specific samples of the sampled data.

Example 1: The Observation captures a snippet of a waveform from an ECG. The waveform has information about a pacemaker beat. It is important to display the exact position of the beat in the waveform for clinical interpretation.

Example 2: The Observation captures a snippet of a waveform from a Ventilator. The waveform has information about the start and end of breathing cycle.

One possibility to provide a means would be to extend the Observation.component with a possibility to indicate the sample(s) that this component observation applies. The Observation.component.code would indicate that this is an annotation, the Observation.component.valueSampledData would include the kind of the annotation (e.g. Pacer beat, Insp. Start).

### 14. Corey Spears - 2024-12-02

We will define an extension with a context of Observation.focus (about an observation that is a waveform) or Observation.note (a note about a particular place in the Waveform)

If your observation coded allows it, you can have a separate dimension in the data that describes the characteristics in the form of coded values. This will allow one to do something like indicate where a peak starts and where it ends. But this can only be done if it is part of the waveform definition.

For other cases, you can make an extension with a context of Observation.focus (e.g. as an Observation about an observation containing the sampleData) or Observation.note (when the notation is considered part of the same observation) indicating a sample number or sample range and a dimension number or dimension range.

Additionally, the has been some initial exploration regarding the representation of ECGs in FHIR, but the topic might warrant more definition/guidance, perhaps through an IG.

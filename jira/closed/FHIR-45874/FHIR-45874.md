- **Description**: After analyzing the extensions used in several countries, it seems that the need for "classification" is common, and is used inconsistently.
https://github.com/IHE/pharm-mpd/wiki/Extensions#product-classification

Classifications like ATC may sometimes be added as a .code (which is not really correct, as ATC is a "grouper" not a product code of any level),
Other classifications are country-specific.

This variance harms interoperability and product identification.

Request to add classification to the Medication resource.

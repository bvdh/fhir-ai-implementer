- **Resolution Description**: Recommendation:

Rewrite the opening sentence of Section 2.8.7 to remove the tautology and disambiguate the term. Suggested replacement:

"Typically, a composition describes a single subject — referenced by Composition.subject — such as a patient, group of patients, location, or device. Some kinds of documents, however, contain data about additional persons or entities related to the composition's subject."This phrasing names the element (Composition.subject) explicitly the first time, drops the broken "distinction between" clause, and sets up the multi-entity discussion that follows without circularity.

Revise the remainder of Section 2.8.7 to qualify each subsequent use of "subject" with which referent is meant:

"the subject of the document" → "the composition's subject (Composition.subject)"
"the subject of the composition is the focus" → "the composition's subject (Composition.subject) is treated as the implicit focus"
"specify that their subject (however named e.g. patient) or focus element" → "specify that their own subject element (such as Observation.subject, Condition.subject, or the resource-specific named equivalent) or focus element"
"compositions genuinely cover multiple subjects" → "compositions genuinely cover multiple distinct subjects across sections"

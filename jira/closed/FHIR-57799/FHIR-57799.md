- **Description**: The following line of text appears at the top of the profile page for the base profile for vital signs:
This profile was published on Sat, Aug 11, 2018 00:00+0000 as a draft by HL7 International / Orders and Observations.

It appears also on all other vital signs profile pages.

This line of text is confusing and not needed. I suggest to remove it from these pages.

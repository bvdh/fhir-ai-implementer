- **Related Artifact(s)**: AllergyIntolerance
- **Resolution Description**: Rename from lastOccurrence to lastReactionOccurrence (with no change to definition)
- **Resolution Vote**: Russ Leftwich / Stephen Chu : 3-2-3
- **Vote Date**: 2024-03-07
- **Change Category**: Clarification
- **Change Impact**: Non-compatible
- **Applied for Version**: R6
- **Description**: lastOccurrence - as per structure definition, is a direct child data element of AllergyIntolerance resource.
This structural design gives rise to interpretation that the lastOccurrence data element describes the last occurence of AllergyIntolerance (notwithstanding its definition states that this data element is about "Date(/time) of last known occurrence of a reaction"
As such, this lastOccurrence structurally should be a child data element of the Reaction backboneElement.
Such change will be a disruptive, non-compatible structure definition change.

Suggestion (as a compromise):
Rename "lastOccurrence" to "reactionLastOccurrence" - to semantic clarity

My priority is always the patient, and the ambiguity of lastOccurrence is a patient safety and patient outcomes risk.

The suggestion of clarifying this in the comments is unworkable and relates to several considerations around the resource.

My experience with now hundreds of developers, students, and implementers is that they do not read the comments. At all.
The value of this element is about clinical workflow and knowledge. A majority of stakeholders do not understand allergy/intolerance or the difference between the entry being a substance to which the patient has a propensity to future reaction. It is common to conflate this with the reaction itself. Entering something in the comment field is not going to resolve this.
There has been a shift from the end user of FHIR being a developer to the end user being the care team, including the patient. This is a shift that is continuing. This increases the importance that the name of this element (and other clinical elements) be clear and unambiguous. The end user is in the care setting, not a software company or IT department.
With the continuing evolution of agentic AI and AI coding, “developer” no longer means what it did a decade ago. It now means members of the care team with domain knowledge and the ability to describe the problem, a proposed solution, and an accurate model of the data. That domain knowledge requires having an unambiguous data model for the allergy/intolerance reaction and the implications for the patient. If the reinforcement data being provided by the care team and the context data for CDS is ambiguous it is sub-optimal care for patients.

### 6. Michelle Miller - 2024-03-07

Patient Care FHIR Conference Call on March 7, 2024

Discussion about whether lastOccurrence should be in AllergyIntolerance.reaction BackboneElement

lastOccurrence is across all reactions (last one)
Each reaction may have different morphologies (subjects can have different reactions to the same substance, which have different risk implications)
Why is lastOccurrence needed if implementers can loop through the reactions list and find the latest / most current onset date/time?

Because reaction requires manifestation, then lastOccurrence allows the resource to say "last occurrence of a reaction - without known manifestation - is on a given date"
Use case - patient can describe when the reaction happened, but not what it was

Comment says "This date may be replicated by one of the Onset of Reaction dates. Where a textual representation of the date of last occurrence is required e.g. 'In Childhood, '10 years ago' the AllergyIntolerance.note element should be used"
Should we remove lastOccurrence?
Should AllergyIntolerance.reaction.onset be renamed occurrence?
Should onset/occurrence be a Period and not a dateTime?
Is renaming the element worth the implementer pain of a non-compatible change?

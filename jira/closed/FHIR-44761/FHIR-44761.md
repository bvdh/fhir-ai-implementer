There is no constraint on ElementDefinition.type.profile or ElementDefinition.type.targetProfile forcing uniqueness. I believe there should be.

I already filed https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-44760 for OO, but having repetitions in profiles or target profiles is noise for readers.

Request adding constraints for those elements to enforce uniqueness.

Thanks!

- **Resolution Description**: Will change the quoted paragraph to:

"When the metadata capabilities section is defined at TestScript.metadata, and the server's conformance statement does not contain the elements defined in the minimal conformance statement, then all the tests in the TestScript are skipped. The "metadata.capabilities.required" and "metadata.capabilities.validated" elements only indicate whether the capabilities are the primary focus of the test script or not. They do not impact the skipping logic. Capabilities whose "metadata.capabilities.validated" flag is true are the primary focus of the test script."
- **Resolution Vote**: Richard Ettema/Gino Canessa: 7-0-0
- **Vote Date**: 2023-12-18
- **Change Category**: Correction
- **Change Impact**: Non-substantive
- **Description**: The comments states the following"
"When the metadata capabilities section is defined at TestScript.metadata or at TestScript.setup.metadata, and the server's conformance statement does not contain the elements defined in the minimal conformance statement, then all the tests in the TestScript are skipped. When the metadata capabilities section is defined at TestScript.test.metadata and the server's conformance statement does not contain the elements defined in the minimal conformance statement, then only that test is skipped. The "metadata.capabilities.required" and "metadata.capabilities.validated" elements only indicate whether the capabilities are the primary focus of the test script or not. They do not impact the skipping logic. Capabilities whose "metadata.capabilities.validated" flag is true are the primary focus of the test script."
Testscript.test.metadata and Testscript.setup.metadata do not appear to exist.

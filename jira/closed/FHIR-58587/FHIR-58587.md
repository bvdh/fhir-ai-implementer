- **Resolution Description**: An event (i.e. any change to current patient status) that may be related to unintended effects on a patient or research participant. The unintended effects may require additional monitoring, treatment, hospitalization, or may result in death. The AdverseEvent resource also extends to potential or avoided events that could have had such effects. There are two major domains where the AdverseEvent resource is expected to be used. Specifically, one domain is in clinical care reported adverse events and the other is in reporting adverse events in clinical research trials management. In clinical trials, an adverse event is any untoward medical occurrence associated with the use of a product in humans, whether or not considered product related. Adverse events can be reported by healthcare providers, patients, caregivers or by medical products manufacturers. Given the differences between these two concepts, we recommend consulting the domain specific implementation guides when implementing the AdverseEvent Resource. The implementation guides include specific extensions, value sets and constraints.

- **Description**: Suggest including the FDA's IND definition in 21 CFR 312.32(a) for research subjects.
"any untoward medical occurrence associated with the use of a drug in humans, whether or not considered drug related."
FDA does not require that the intervention caused the event. In FDA terminology, causality starts distinguishing an AE from a suspected adverse reaction or adverse reaction:
AE: temporal association; causality not required.
Suspected adverse reaction: reasonable possibility that the drug caused it.
Adverse reaction: event caused by the drug.

"One is in clinical care reported adverse events and the other is in reporting adverse events in clinical research trial management. "
Suggest revising to:" One is in clinical care reported adverse events and the other is in reporting adverse events in clinical trials."

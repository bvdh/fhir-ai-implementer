- **Resolution Description**: Will replace the set of codes with the following:

System: A software application or collection of such applications functioning as or represented as a single entity. (This would be instantiated in FHIR as a Device.)
Non-system: A person, animal, (non-software) device, organization or other entity

Individual: A person or animal

Patient

Practitioner

RelatedPerson

Device: A physical device acting in non-software capacity
Collective: A group of people or animals

CareTeam

Group

HealthcareService

Organization

Those without definitions will take the short definitions of the associated resources.

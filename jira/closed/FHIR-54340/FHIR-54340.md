### 3. Arianne van de Wetering - 2026-04-29

The word 'details' implies that the concept has details of a Dosage, however Dosage is a repeating concept within DosageDetails. In fact DosageDetails has repeating step which has repeating Dosage.

That does not make sense. Dosage seems too small of a concept when we also convey route and additional instructions (do not to take it with grapefruit) and so on.

The current concept (with name "DosageDetails") contains all the information a person needs to find out how the medication is/was/should be taken. In Dutch we have the name 'Gebruiksinstructie' for this concept, which we also use for the is/was usecase. We have translated 'Gebruiksinstructie' to InstructionsForUse, however I do appreciate that for the is/was usecase the word 'instruction' may be a bit less appropriate. UsageDetails maybe?

Our current model: https://zibs.nl/wiki/InstructionsForUse-v4.0(2026-1EN)

### 4. Jose Costa-Teixeira - 2026-04-13

Why do you think this is necessary? Naming can change because this is a new element structure but InstructionsForUse doesn't seem a good name and we should not iterate much on this

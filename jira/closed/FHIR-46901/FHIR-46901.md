- **Description**: Reading the resource Medication, I understand it can be used to represent 3 types of Medications:

1. Generic Medications/Formulations: Paracetamol 500mg tablets

2. Commercial Medications: Tylenol 500mg tablets (including batch information)

3. Compounded Medication/Preparations (aka extemporaneous or magistral- product that is manufactured by the pharmacy at the time of dispensing): HC 1%, Salicylic Acid 5% in Glaxal Base

I understand that filtering/search medicines based on their types (one of the 3 above) is important for healthcare applications. For example, a physician during the prescription of a medication in the CPOE normally will NOT search for compounded preparations created for a specific patient, but will search for generic medications/formulations (e.g., paracetamol) and commercial medications.

So, how to differentiate the different types of medicines? How to query only Generic Medications/Formulations or Commercial Medications?

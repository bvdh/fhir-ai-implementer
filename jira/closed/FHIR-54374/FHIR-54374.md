- **Resolution Description**: The dispense part of administration does not necessarily mean that there will be a circular reference - those would be different cases - in no case a specific instance of administration and an instance of dispense would have a circular reference.

But we can't exclude cases where the Dispense is part of Administration - e.g. a dispense from ward stock or emergency cart, where there is no formal, separate dispensation record, but we don't want to disallow the capture of an implicit "dispense" - stating that in order to administer the emergency medication, there was a dispense from the emergency cart - this can be allowed e.g. for having Dispense handling the logistics / inventory aspects without burdening Administration with that.

We will add the above text (adapted) in the comments of MedicationDispense.partOf.

- **Description**: MedicationDispense can reference MedicationAdministration it's "part of."

Unclear use case: When is a dispense "part of" an administration? Usually, it's the reverse - administration follows dispensing.

This also creates potential circular references:

MedicationDispense.partOf → MedicationAdministration
MedicationAdministration (references dispense in some way)

Propose removing MedicationAdministration as a target or adding a rationale for its inclusion in the comments.

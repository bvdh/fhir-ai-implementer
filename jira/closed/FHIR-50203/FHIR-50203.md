- **Description**: This ballot introduces the concept of https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot3/resource.html#additional (Additional resources defined outside of FHIR). The use of "additional resources" on the search page is referring to resources other than that searched for. It is confusing. Consider changing name of "Additional Resources" to something like "FHIR+ Resources" or some other name.

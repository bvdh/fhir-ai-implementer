- **Resolution Description**: Add CareTeam and Group as participants

1. CareTeam is allowed as an Author but not as a Participant

2. Group is allowed as an Attester/Custodian but not as a Participant

However, after inspecting the participant types, CareTeam and Group should logically be recorded as participants. For example, participant.type of primary information recipient, tracker, and information recipient. Note that both participant.type and participant.function are preferred bindings, so other concepts could be used that logically align with CareTeam and Group as targets.

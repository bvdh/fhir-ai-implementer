- **Description**: Why must the definition of the other actor be in another IG, why not in the same IG, why not outside an IG? What dependencies are being referenced here? There is no dependencies element in the ActorDefinition. Again, why talk about obligations in the other IG, rather than obligations of the other actor?

Existing Wording:
A url that identifies the definition of this actor in another IG (which IG must be listed in the dependencies). This actor inherits all the obligations etc. as defined in the other IG.

- **Description**: The Dosage Regime Examples https://hl7.org/fhir/6.0.0-ballot4/dosage-examples.html contain examples where the textual representation does not match with the structural representation.

Example 1 for instance has a dosage " 1 tablet per day for three months" and a medication.concept "Perindopril Arginine, 2.5". The structure for this example is:

dosageInstruction
renderedInstruction: 1 tablet per day for 3 months
...
...
doseAndRate
doseQuantity: 10 mg

If the medication refers to 2.5mg tablets, and the instruction states 1 tablet per day, shouldn't the doseQuantity be 2.5mg? 10mg is equal to 4 tablets and not 1 tablet.

Suggestion to change structure (and json/xml) to either

MedicationRequest

medication

concept

coding: Perindopril Arginine, 2.5mg tablets

dosageInstruction

renderedInstruction: 1 tablet per day for 3 months

simple

timing

repeat

boundsDuration: 3 mo

frequency : 1

period: 1

periodUnit: d

doseAndRate

doseQuantity: 2.5 mg

or

MedicationRequest

medication

concept

coding: Perindopril Arginine, 2.5mg tablets

dosageInstruction

renderedInstruction: 1 tablet per day for 3 months

simple

timing

repeat

boundsDuration: 3 mo

frequency : 1

period: 1

periodUnit: d

doseAndRate

doseQuantity: 1 tablet

- **Description**: ServiceRequest.reasons (or indications for imaging studies) includes a lot of potential triggers/justifications. E.g. A completed procedure that merits follow-up imaging, a family history detail that warrants investigation, a medication administration that warrants checking for effects/side-effects, etc.

Presumably the wide range of possible services that could be requested by have a similarly wide range of possible reasons/triggers/motivations.

Proposal: Consider changing to Reference (Any). Or at least include Procedure, FamilyHistory, MedicationAdministration.

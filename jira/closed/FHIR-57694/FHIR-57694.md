- **Resolution Description**: Consent enforcement is the process of ensuring that the rules of pertinent consents are applied to data access (or other actions such a procedure in the case of treatment consents). This requires (a) identifying the parameters of the action and its context (e.g., the actor, the purpose of use, the requested data, etc.), (b) finding consents that are applicable to the context of requested access, (c) evaluating the consent rules against the context of access to reach a decision, and (d) ensuring that the decision is applied (e.g., data access is permitted or denied). The first three steps are sometimes recognized as a distinct function, known as "consent decision."

Common data access control mechanisms and technologies (e.g., OAuth and SMART-on-FHIR scopes, XACML, ODRL, etc.) may be leveraged in the process of consent enforcement. The details of consent enforcement is often deeply entangled with the specifics of the data access workflow and technology stack, therefore generally out of the scope this specification.

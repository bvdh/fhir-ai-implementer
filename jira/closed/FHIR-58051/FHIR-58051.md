- **Description**: The information that is expected in Composition.type versus .category is not clear and it brings confusion among implementers. Moreover the value set's that are bound are constructed almost the same way "Include codes from http://loinc.org icon version Not Stated (use latest from terminology server) where SCALE_TYP = LP32888-7 (Doc)" .

The definitions are also not very helpful:

A categorization for the type of the composition - helps with indexing and searching. This may be implied by or derived from the code specified in the Composition Type.

Is category expected to be a subtype of the type, or is it an independent dimension?

Please provide clearer definitions and, if possible complement with examples of types and categories.

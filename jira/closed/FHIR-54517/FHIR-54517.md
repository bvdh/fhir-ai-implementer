- **Resolution Description**: The Sentence "Any additional documents derived from the same Composition SHALL have a different Bundle.identifier." Does not see to add any additional information than what is conveyed in an above statement "The document identifier (mandatory) found in Bundle.identifier. It is globally unique for this instance of the document, and is never re-used, including for other documents derived from the same composition"

We will remove the sentence.

"Any additional documents derived from the same Composition SHALL have a different Bundle.identifier."

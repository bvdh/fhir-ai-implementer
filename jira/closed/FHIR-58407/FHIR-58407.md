- **Description**: The definition around both elements talk about devices that generate measurements. It would be better to either consolidate the elements or provide specific guidance on usage. One approach would be to align with the Procedure.focalDevice and Procedure.used elements, which would allow you to articulate incidental use of a device while generating the Observation versus the focal reason for the Observation.
